            Setup

            Set once, in daylight, by someone awake.

            Intervals and daily ceilings are fixed by the preset you choose here. They are deliberately not adjustable from the instrument face — a safety ceiling is not something a half-asleep parent should be able to raise at 3am. Choose the preset that matches the country your medicine was bought in, then leave it alone.

                Read this every time

                Not medical advice and not a substitute for the label on your bottle. Always check the concentration and dose printed on the packaging, and follow your clinician or pharmacist. Seek urgent medical help for any red-flag symptom.

                Dose computes volumes from the weight and the bottle strength you enter, and records what you tell it you gave. Reference growth curves are approximations of published WHO and CDC data, shown for context only — your clinician’s chart is the definitive one. Under 3 months old, any fever is a reason to be seen the same day; do not use a calculator instead of a phone call.

                  If any of these are true, stop using this tool and phone your emergency number or go to urgent care now.
