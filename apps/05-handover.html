Why this rots on purposePhone numbers expire after 90 days, medical detail after 12 months, and the whole brief expires the moment a child crosses a dosing band. A brief that looks current but isn't is worse than no brief.

Section six

Issue a document

Same record, six documents. Tick one and print — only the pages that edition needs will come out.

The August peakMost families issue the school form once a year, the sideline card at the start of a season, and the sitter brief a dozen times. The record is entered once.

Please read. This is a convenience document you prepare yourself. It is not a legal instrument; check what your childcare provider or school requires. Medicine figures are arithmetic on the weight and strengths you typed — always check them against the label on the bottle and against what your GP or pharmacist has told you. In an emergency, call the emergency services first.
