This is a record of a visit and a charge calculation — it is not a safety or compliance certificate. The sheet totals the time, travel and parts you enter at the rates you set. It does not inspect anything, does not decide whether an appliance or installation is safe, and does not replace the statutory paperwork: gas work needs a Gas Safe registered engineer and the appropriate gas safety record, electrical work needs the relevant certificate or minor works form, and refrigerant work needs an F-Gas logbook entry. Any tax shown is arithmetic at the rate you type, not tax advice. A customer signature here confirms the work described was carried out and the time recorded is correct; it is not a waiver of the customer's statutory rights or of any warranty.

Service Call Sheet. Time on site for each visit is the time off site minus the time on site (a finish time earlier than the start is treated as running past midnight), minus the break. That time is rounded up to the billing increment you choose, then charged as hours × labour rate × band multiplier, each visit rounded to two decimals. Travel minutes are rounded up the same way and charged at the travel rate with the same band multiplier. The minimum charge is minimum hours × labour rate × the highest band multiplier used on site; if the labour lines come to less than that, the difference is added as a minimum-charge uplift — travel, parts and additional charges sit outside the minimum. Parts marked to order are listed for follow-up and are never charged. Under a service contract labour and travel are zero-rated and parts are charged; a warranty job is zero throughout. Tax is charged on the subtotal at the rate you enter. Everything is stored in this browser only — nothing is uploaded, and clearing your browser data deletes it.
