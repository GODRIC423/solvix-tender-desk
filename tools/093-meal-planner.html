An estimate from your own prices, not a supermarket quote. Costs are your unit prices multiplied by the rounded-up pack quantities on the list; they take no account of offers, multibuys, pack sizes or substitutions, and the real till total will differ. This planner does not check nutrition, allergens or dietary suitability — if anyone you are cooking for has an allergy or a medical diet, read the actual product labels and take advice from a dietitian or your GP.

Pantry staples

—

Tick what you already have enough of. Ticked staples drop off the shopping list and out of the estimated total — the quantity the week needs is still shown so you can check the jar.

Ingredients are scaled by cooked portions ÷ recipe base servings, aggregated across the whole plan and converted to a single base unit per ingredient (grams for weight, millilitres for volume, whole items for anything counted), so 500 g + 0.5 kg totals 1 kg. Conversions used: 1 kg = 1 000 g, 1 lb = 453.59237 g, 1 oz = 28.349523125 g, 1 L = 1 000 ml, 1 US qt = 946.353 ml, 1 US fl oz = 29.5735 ml, 1 cup = 240 ml, 1 tbsp = 15 ml, 1 tsp = 5 ml. Everything is stored only in this browser — nothing is uploaded. Estimates only; check labels for allergens.
