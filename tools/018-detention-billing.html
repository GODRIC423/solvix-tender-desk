It does not decide whether a charge is payable: detention, layover, TONU and accessorial pay only apply where your rate confirmation, broker/shipper accessorial policy or carrier agreement allows them, and most require timestamps evidenced on the BOL or by gate/ELD records within the notice window. There is no government-set detention rate. Verify against the rate confirmation and, for disputes, take legal or brokerage advice.

Charge summary

Time on site across all stops: —

Detention = (time on site − free time) per stop, rounded by the chosen increment rule, × the hourly rate, then capped at the daily maximum by arrival date where one is set (a cap is shared pro rata across that day's stops). Layover and TONU are flat charges; every other accessorial is quantity × rate. Money is rounded to 2 decimal places line by line so the printed invoice adds up exactly. Timestamps are taken as entered in local time, with no time-zone conversion between stops. Free time, rates, rounding and caps are contract terms, not legal minimums — enter what your rate confirmation says. All data stays in this browser.
