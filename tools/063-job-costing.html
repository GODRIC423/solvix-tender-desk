Gross margin = (price − direct cost) ÷ price. Net margin = (price − direct cost − overhead) ÷ price. Markup applied = net profit ÷ total cost. Break-even price = total cost. Price at target margin = total cost ÷ (1 − target ÷ 100). Labour true cost = hours × wage rate × (1 + on-costs ÷ 100). Overhead is recovered either at a rate per labour hour or as a percentage of direct cost, as selected. Money is rounded to two decimals for display only; every total is computed from the unrounded figures. All figures exclude VAT and other sales taxes. No standard costing framework is implied — this is an internal management calculation, not an accounting statement.
