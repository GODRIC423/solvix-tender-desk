Counted cash = Σ (face value × quantity) over every denomination. Expected cash = opening float + cash takings − paid-outs − banked during the day. Variance = counted − expected; positive is cash over, negative is cash short. The tolerance applied is the greater of the fixed amount and the stated percentage of cash takings. Sum of tenders = cash + card terminals + vouchers, account sales and other tenders; the Z-reading is reconciled against that sum. Amount to bank = counted cash − float retained, and the suggested float make-up is an exact split of the notes and coins counted where one exists. All money is held in whole minor units (pence/cents) and shown to 2 decimal places; quantities are whole pieces. Data is saved in this browser only. Not accounting, audit or tax advice.
