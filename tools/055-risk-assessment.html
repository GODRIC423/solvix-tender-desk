This tool does not carry out the risk assessment — you do

Every hazard, control and score below is your judgement. The tool only multiplies, bands and totals what it is given, so a low score here is not evidence that a risk is actually low. It is not legal advice and it certifies compliance with nothing. The assessment must be made by a competent person who knows the work, the site and the people exposed, and reviewed whenever anything changes.

For a significant or specialist hazard — asbestos, confined spaces, explosive atmospheres, lifting operations, hazardous substances, machinery guarding — take advice from a qualified health and safety professional before work starts. Nothing here creates or discharges a duty under the Management of Health and Safety at Work Regulations 1999, the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974, or the equivalent legislation in any other country.

Method. Risk score = likelihood (1–5) × severity (1–5), giving 1–25, banded Low 1–4, Medium 5–9, High 10–16, Extreme 17–25. The residual score uses the likelihood and severity you judge will remain once the additional controls are actually in place; the tool compares the two bands and reports whether the control moved the hazard down a band, left it where it was, or made it worse. An extreme residual score is flagged as not acceptable to proceed.

The 5×5 matrix and these band thresholds are a widely used industry convention, not a legal standard — HSE guidance INDG163 Risk assessment: a brief guide does not require a numerical matrix at all. Check the bands and the required actions against your own organisation's health and safety policy before issuing this document. Nothing is sent anywhere: all data stays in this browser on this device.
