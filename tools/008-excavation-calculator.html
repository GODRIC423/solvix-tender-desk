Sample take-off loaded. Foundations, drainage and a driveway on one plot — so every figure works end to end. Type straight over it, or start clean.
Estimate only — not an excavation design. This sheet converts the dimensions you enter into bank, loose and compacted volumes using the bulking and shrinkage factors shown. It is not a temporary-works design and it does not tell you whether a face is safe. Battering, benching, shoring and trench support must be designed by a competent person to the rules that apply on your site — in Great Britain CDM 2015 with HSG185, in the United States OSHA 29 CFR 1926 Subpart P. Bulking and shrinkage vary widely with moisture, plant and compaction effort: confirm against the geotechnical report or a site trial before you commit to haulage rates or a disposal price. Check for buried services and contaminated ground before any dig.

Solvix Toolkit · 008 — Excavation Cut & Fill. Bank volume from the dimensions entered: vertical sides L×W×d, battered trench L×d×(W+s·d), battered pit d×(L·W+s·d·(L+W)+⁴⁄₃·s²·d²) — the prismoidal formula for a rectangular frustum. Loose volume = bank × bulking factor; compacted fill = loose ÷ shrink factor; loads = ⌈loose ÷ truck capacity⌉. Default bulking factors are the customary trade values (sand 1.10, gravel 1.12, clay 1.30, rock 1.50) and every one of them is editable. Excavation support and slope stability are outside this tool: see HSE HSG185 and CDM 2015 (GB) or OSHA 29 CFR 1926 Subpart P (US). Works offline; your data stays in this browser.
