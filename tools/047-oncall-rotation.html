Planning aid only — not a working-time or pay compliance check. This tool allocates on-call cover fairly and shows who holds the pager. It does not check rest periods, maximum weekly hours, night-work limits or standby pay, it does not know your public holidays (it uses only the dates you enter), and it is not a call-out or escalation system. Before you publish a rota, check it against the contracts of employment, any collective or works agreement, and the working-time rules that apply where your people are based — take advice from your HR or legal adviser if in doubt.
