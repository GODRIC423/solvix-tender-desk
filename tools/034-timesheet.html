Worked time per shift = (end − start, carried past midnight when the end is at or before the start) − unpaid break minutes, rounded to the increment you choose. Daily bands are applied to each day's hours in order — regular, then the ×1.5 band, then the ×2 band — and the weekly threshold is then tested against the hours still counted as regular, so no hour is counted in two bands. Pay = regular × rate + overtime × rate × OT multiplier + double time × rate × DT multiplier, each band rounded to 2 decimal places. Thresholds and multipliers are the ones you enter, not statutory values. Gross pay before any deduction. Data is saved only in this browser. Not payroll, tax or legal advice.
