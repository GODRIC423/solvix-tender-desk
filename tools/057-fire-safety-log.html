What this document is. A maintenance record only. It lists the routine test intervals commonly recommended by BS 5306-3/-8, BS 5839-1, BS 5266-1 / BS EN 50172, BS EN 671-3, BS 9990, BS 7346-8, BS EN 12845 and the Fire Safety (England) Regulations 2022, and computes each next-due date from the last test recorded here. It is not a fire risk assessment and does not certify that any equipment is fit for use. Intervals may be varied by your fire risk assessment, your insurer or the manufacturer's instructions, and the law that applies to your premises may differ. Servicing must be carried out by a competent person. Consult your fire risk assessor, your service provider or the fire and rescue authority.

Fire Safety Equipment Log. Next-due dates are computed as last recorded test + the standard interval for that equipment type (BS 5306-3 and BS 5306-8 for extinguishers, blankets and hose reels; BS 5839-1 for fire alarm systems and manual call points; BS 5266-1 / BS EN 50172 for emergency lighting; BS EN 671-3, BS 9990, BS 7346-8 and BS EN 12845 for hose reels, dry risers, smoke vents and sprinklers; the Fire Safety (England) Regulations 2022 for fire door checks). Where no test has been recorded, the interval runs from the install date. Months are calendar months, clamped to the end of shorter months.
