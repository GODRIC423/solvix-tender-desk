What this is. A reconciliation worksheet: it adds up only what you type in and compares the tin count with the book balance. It is not an audit, and it is not tax, payroll or accounting advice. Keep every voucher, and have a second person witness the count. Check the figures against your own records before posting them or drawing cash.
Saved in this browser only.

Imprest system: the tin is topped back up to a fixed float each period, so the top-up required is float − current balance. Book balance = opening balance + top-ups in − payments out. Counted cash = Σ (face value × quantity) across every denomination. Variance = counted cash − book balance; a positive variance is cash over, a negative variance is cash short. All money is held and totalled in whole minor units (pence/cents) and shown to 2 decimal places. Denomination sets are the everyday circulating notes and coins for the selected currency and can be edited. Saved only in this browser. Not tax, payroll or accounting advice.
