Licensee & filing details

Interest & penalty (only if filing late)

Under IFTA Articles of Agreement R1230 interest accrues each month on the tax due to each jurisdiction — never on a credit — at one twelfth of the annual rate set for the year. Confirm the current rate and your base jurisdiction's late-filing penalty (commonly the greater of a flat fee or a percentage of net tax due) before you rely on these figures.

Payment reference
Assumes one fuel type (diesel) across the fleet. Average fleet MPG = total miles ÷ total gallons, rounded to 2 decimal places; taxable gallons = taxable miles ÷ that MPG, rounded to 3 decimal places; tax = net taxable gallons × the rate you enter. Interest per IFTA Articles of Agreement R1230 is applied monthly to tax owed to a jurisdiction and never to a credit. Rates are never built in — take them from the IFTA Tax Rate Matrix (IFTA, Inc., iftach.org) for the quarter you are filing. Runs entirely offline; your data is saved only in this browser.
