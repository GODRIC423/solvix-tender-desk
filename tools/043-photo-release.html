Not legal advice — read before anyone signs. This tool assembles a plain-English photo, model and property release from the details you enter, and works out the licence dates, the subject's age and which signatures the form needs. It is a drafting aid. It is not a lawyer, it does not know where the images will be published, and it cannot confirm the release is valid or enforceable where you are. Image and personality rights differ by country and by US state; a release signed by or for a person under the age of majority can be challenged or disaffirmed in some jurisdictions; and a signed release is not by itself a lawful basis under UK GDPR, the EU GDPR or comparable data-protection law. Have the release checked by a solicitor or attorney qualified where the images will be used, and keep the signed original with proof of identity and, for a minor, proof of the guardian's authority. Uses such as stock resale, sensitive-product advertising and AI or machine-learning training carry particular risk and should be taken separately.
