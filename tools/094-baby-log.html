Times are your device's local clock. A sleep that crosses midnight is split between the two days by the overlap with each day, so daily sleep totals always add up. Night sleep is the part of sleep falling inside your night window; daytime sleep is the rest. Conversions: 1 fl oz (US) = 29.5735 ml, 1 oz = 28.3495 g, °F = °C × 9/5 + 32. Everything is stored only in this browser — nothing is sent anywhere. This is a record-keeping tool: it performs no medical assessment of any kind.
