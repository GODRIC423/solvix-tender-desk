Verification is the operator’s responsibility. This matrix reports only what has been entered into it — it is not checked against any supplier specification, and “free from” here means “not present in the ingredients recorded”, not “guaranteed absent”. Confirm every ingredient against the current supplier specification, re-check after any recipe or supplier change, and always speak to the guest before serving. A shared kitchen — shared fryers, oil, grills, utensils or surfaces — can transfer any allergen, so no dish can be treated as free from an allergen on the strength of its recipe alone. Recipe changes, substitutions and supplier reformulation are not detected by this document. Not legal advice — for your obligations consult your environmental health officer or food safety adviser.

Guest allergen check

Columns are the 14 allergens named in Annex II of Regulation (EU) No 1169/2011, retained in UK law and enforced through the Food Information Regulations 2014 — sulphur dioxide and sulphites are declarable at concentrations above 10 mg/kg or 10 mg/litre expressed as SO₂. A dish inherits the highest status of any ingredient in it (contains beats may contain beats free from), plus any cross-contact you record for its preparation. Statuses are those entered by the operator; nothing here is verified automatically. Food prepacked for direct sale must also carry a full ingredient list (Natasha’s Law) — this matrix does not replace that label.
