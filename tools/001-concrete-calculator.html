Estimate only. This tool converts the dimensions you enter into volume and multiplies by published bag yields. It does not verify your dimensions, structural design, reinforcement, subgrade, formwork movement or local building code. Confirm the final quantity and mix specification with your supplier and your engineer or building official before ordering or placing concrete.
— Materials always rounded up
Volumes: slab and footing L×W×D, column π r² h, steps summed as stacked slabs (bottom tread spans the full run). Conversions: 1 yd³ = 27 ft³, 1 ft³ = 0.0283168 m³. Bag yields are the manufacturers' nominal figures — 40 lb = 0.30 ft³, 60 lb = 0.45 ft³, 80 lb = 0.60 ft³ — and bag counts are rounded up. Ready-mix is rounded up to the next 0.25 yd³ as supplied by most plants. Estimating aid only; not a structural design and not a substitute for your supplier's or engineer's advice.
