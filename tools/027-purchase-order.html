Read before you send this order. A purchase order is a commercial offer that becomes a binding contract once your supplier accepts it. This tool builds and totals the document from the figures you type. It does not check your supplier's terms of sale, it does not verify that the tax rates you enter are the correct ones for those goods, that supplier or that country of supply, and it does not enforce your organisation's delegated-authority limits — the budget check below compares your own numbers only. Confirm tax treatment with your finance team or accountant, and have the order approved by someone authorised to commit the spend before issuing it.

Line net = quantity × unit cost. Line tax = line net × tax rate ÷ 100, each line rounded to 2 decimal places before summing. PO total = goods net + carriage + total tax. Outstanding = (quantity ordered − quantity received) × unit cost. Budget remaining = budget limit − PO total on the basis you select. Everything is stored in this browser only (localStorage) — nothing is sent anywhere. Tax rates, delegated authority limits and supplier terms are yours to verify.
