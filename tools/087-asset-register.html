Assumptions: net book value calculated from elapsed time since purchase to date of printing (or disposal date where disposed), using a 365.25-day year. Straight-line = (cost − residual) ÷ life, applied evenly. Reducing balance = cost × (1 − rate)^years, floored at residual value. Generated by the Solvix Asset Register tool.

This tool assumes a flat 365.25-day year for elapsed-time depreciation, floors net book value at the entered residual value, and treats reducing-balance rate as a straight input (not auto-derived). QR labels encode the asset tag, an internal lookup reference, serial and location as plain text — any standard QR reader will display it; no internet lookup is performed.
