This is a price quotation, not a contract or tax advice. It totals only the figures you type in and applies the tax rate you set to each line. It does not check your tax registration, the correct rate or place of supply, whether the work is priced viably, or whether your terms are enforceable. Check tax treatment with your accountant and have your terms of business reviewed by a solicitor before you send quotes out. Once signed, a quotation and its terms can form a binding contract.
