Implements the federal hours-of-service limits for property-carrying commercial drivers in 49 CFR 395.3: 11 hours driving and a 14-hour driving window after 10 consecutive hours off duty (§395.3(a)); a 30-minute interruption of driving after 8 cumulative driving hours (§395.3(a)(3)(ii)); 60 hours in 7 days or 70 hours in 8 days (§395.3(b)) with the optional 34-consecutive-hour restart (§395.3(c)); and the sleeper-berth split of §395.1(g)(1) — a period of at least 7 consecutive hours in the sleeper berth paired with a separate period of at least 2 consecutive hours off duty or in the sleeper berth, totalling at least 10 hours, with neither period counting against the 14-hour window and the clocks recalculated from the end of the first period of the pair. Events are treated as consecutive with no unaccounted gaps. Exceptions and exemptions are not modelled. Runs entirely offline; your data is saved only in this browser.
