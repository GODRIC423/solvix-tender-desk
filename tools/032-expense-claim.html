Tax on each line is computed from the basis and rate you choose — net-up (tax = net × rate) or gross-inclusive (net = gross ÷ (1 + rate)) — and every figure is rounded to 2 decimal places. Mileage lines are distance × rate per mile/km; per-diem lines are days × daily rate. Category limits, the extra-approval threshold and the rates are your organisation's own policy values, not statutory figures. Saved only in this browser. Not tax, payroll or legal advice.
