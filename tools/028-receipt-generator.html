What this is, and what it is not. This tool produces a receipt acknowledging money you say you have received, using only the figures you type in. It does not verify that funds have cleared, it is not a VAT or sales-tax invoice, and it does not calculate any tax. Issue a receipt only once the payment has actually cleared, and check with your accountant or tax authority what records you are required to keep.
