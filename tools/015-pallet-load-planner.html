Cases are assumed rectangular and stacked in uniform layers (single block or split block) on one pallet size per line; no interlocking, no mixed-SKU pallets, no slip sheets or dunnage allowance. Layers are limited by max load height and max stack weight, both measured including the pallet. Trailer positions are computed from the internal dimensions entered, not looked up: the stock figures — 53 ft dry van 636 × 100 × 110 in (26 positions straight, 30 turned), 48 ft 576 × 100 × 108 in (24 straight), 26 ft box 312 × 96 × 96 in (12 straight) — fall out of the same arithmetic. Payload defaults to 45,000 lb, the usual dry-van maximum under an 80,000 lb gross limit. Verify axle weights on a scale.
