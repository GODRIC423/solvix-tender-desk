Check the price against the official U.S. EIA/DOE weekly On-Highway Diesel Fuel Price release for the date shown, and bill only the method, peg and fuel economy your contract provides for. The surcharge method, base peg, fuel economy and any percentage step table are contractual terms; figures on this schedule are a calculation aid, not a statement of the published index. Not tax, legal or accounting advice — if a figure is disputed, agree it with your customer or broker before invoicing.

Implements the standard U.S. per-mile fuel surcharge formula used with the EIA/DOE weekly On-Highway Diesel Fuel Price survey: surcharge per mile = (index price − base peg price) ÷ fuel economy, extended by loaded miles; and the alternative percentage-of-linehaul method, where the index price selects a band in the step table above. Defaults: peg $1.25/gal, 6.0 mpg. Money is rounded to 2 decimal places per load; the per-distance rate is rounded to the precision you choose before it is extended. Metric mode converts gallons at 3.785411784 L and miles at 1.609344 km. Nothing leaves this device — all data is stored in this browser only.
