Waste report

This section is the document — it prints as your waste report.
Value is quantity × unit cost per entry, rounded to the nearest cent. Daily, weekly and period totals sum every logged entry in range. The trend groups entries into Monday–Sunday weeks. A percentage-of-cost or -sales figure only appears once you enter a period figure above, and the target alert only appears once a target is set. This log tracks waste cost only — it is not a food-safety, temperature or allergen record; keep those separately as required. Data is stored only in this browser (localStorage) — export CSV/JSON to back it up or move it to another device.
