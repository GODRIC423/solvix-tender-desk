Waste report

This section is the document — it's what prints.
Weeks run Monday–Sunday based on today's date. Totals use the quantity and unit cost entered per line, rounded to 2 decimal places; entries missing a date are still counted in all-time totals but excluded from daily/weekly grouping. Percentages compare this week's logged waste value against the sales or food cost figure you enter — this log does not connect to your POS or accounts, so treat it as an estimate and reconcile against those records. Data is stored only in this browser (localStorage) — export CSV/JSON to back it up or move it to another device.
