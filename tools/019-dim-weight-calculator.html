Estimate for planning and quoting — not a rate quote. Dimensional divisors are set by each carrier, differ by service and country, and are frequently changed; a negotiated contract often carries a divisor other than the published one. This tool computes billable weight only — it does not price freight, fuel, residential, oversize, additional-handling or any other accessorial charge. Confirm the divisor, the measuring rule and the rounding rule in your current carrier agreement or published rate guide before you invoice a customer or accept a charge.

Basis of calculation. Dimensional weight = (Length × Width × Height) ÷ divisor, computed for each package separately. Billable weight per package = the greater of actual weight and dimensional weight, rounded up to the next whole pound or kilogram, then multiplied by the quantity of identical packages and summed across the shipment.

This document states billable weight only. It does not price freight, fuel, residential, oversize, additional-handling or any other accessorial charge. Estimated cost figures, where shown, are the operator's own rate per unit weight multiplied by billable weight.
Dim weight = (L × W × H) ÷ divisor. Billable weight = greater of actual and dim weight, rounded up to the next whole lb/kg per package, then totalled across the shipment. Divisor defaults follow the published parcel standards — 139 in³/lb (5,000 cm³/kg) for UPS, FedEx and DHL, 166 in³/lb (6,000 cm³/kg) for USPS, and the 250 in³/lb cube rule used for LTL and domestic air freight; the metric LTL figure of 9,000 cm³/kg is the converted equivalent of 250 in³/lb (≈9,032) and is shown rounded — edit it to match your tariff. Every divisor on this page is editable. Conversions: 1 in = 2.54 cm exactly, 1 lb = 0.45359237 kg exactly. All data stays in this browser; nothing is uploaded.
