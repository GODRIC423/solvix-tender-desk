Equipment & term

The discount rate is used two ways: it prices the opportunity cost of cash spent buying outright, and it discounts every option's cash flows to today's money for a fair net-present-value comparison. Resale % applies to Buy and Finance only — a leased asset is handed back with no residual to you.

FINANCE  Financed purchase

Same purchase price, resale value and maintenance as Buy above — financing changes only how the cash goes out.

LEASE  Operating lease

The asset is returned at the end of the lease — no resale value, no opportunity cost of capital, because you never tied up the purchase price.

Tax treatment

This tool does not calculate tax. Capital allowances / depreciation, interest deductibility, VAT or GST recovery, and whether lease payments are fully deductible as an operating expense all depend on your jurisdiction, entity structure and how the asset is used — they can change which option is really cheapest after tax. Record your assumption below and confirm it with your accountant before deciding. Every figure on this page is a pre-tax cash comparison only.
