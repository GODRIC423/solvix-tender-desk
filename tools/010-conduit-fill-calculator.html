Conduit Fill & Wire Ampacity

NEC CH.9 T1 · T4 · T5 · T310.16
The authority having jurisdiction governs — this is a calculation aid, not an approval. Fill comes from NEC Chapter 9 Tables 1, 4 and 5; ampacity from Table 310.16 with Tables 310.15(B)(1) and 310.15(C)(1). It does not cover bends, pull points, conduit bodies, box fill, voltage drop, overcurrent-device sizing or any local amendment. Have the design confirmed by a licensed electrician or engineer before you pull wire — the full basis and limitations are printed on the record.

Step by step fill calculation
StepValueBasis

Fill limits: NEC Chapter 9 Table 1 — one conductor 53 %, two conductors 31 %, over two conductors 40 % (60 % for nipples 600 mm / 24 in or shorter, Note 4). Conductor areas: Chapter 9 Table 5, THHN / THWN / THWN-2, 14 AWG to 500 kcmil. Raceway areas: Chapter 9 Table 4 (EMT, IMC, RMC, PVC Sch. 40). Ampacity: Table 310.16 (not more than three current-carrying conductors, 30 °C ambient), corrected by Table 310.15(B)(1) and adjusted by Table 310.15(C)(1), then limited by the termination rating of 110.14(C). Figures follow the 2020 NEC. Assumes a single raceway, conductors of one insulation family, no conduit bodies, and no local amendment. All data stays in this browser; nothing is uploaded.
