Rates change — check before you claim. This tool applies the mileage rates you select and the tier boundary across your chosen period. It is a record-keeping and arithmetic tool, not tax advice. It does not file anything, does not calculate tax or National Insurance due, and does not decide whether a journey qualifies as business travel. Confirm the current approved rates with HMRC (Approved Mileage Allowance Payments) or the IRS (standard mileage rate) for the year you are claiming, keep the underlying receipts and odometer evidence, and consult your accountant or payroll team before submitting.

Assumes every logged journey is a genuine business journey you can evidence, that distances are actual road distances, and that the tier 1 limit resets at the start of the claim period you set. Implements the HMRC Approved Mileage Allowance Payments structure (car/van 45p for the first 10,000 business miles in the tax year then 25p, motorcycle 24p, cycle 20p, passenger supplement 5p per passenger per mile) and the IRS single standard mileage rate, with a custom two-tier employer scheme as an alternative. Distances convert at 1 mile = 1.609344 km. Money is rounded to the nearest penny per journey; the claim total is the sum of those rounded journey amounts. All data stays in this browser — nothing is uploaded.
