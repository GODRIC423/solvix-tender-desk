This is a starting proposal, not a ruling. Deposit protection schemes (TDS, DPS, mydeposits) adjudicate on evidence and on fair wear & tear — a claim for the full replacement cost of a part-worn item is routinely reduced or rejected outright. Every line here needs a check-in record, photos and, where money changed hands, an invoice to stand any chance at adjudication. This tool does not represent a scheme, a court or legal advice — where the tenant disputes a deduction, use your scheme's free dispute resolution service before deducting or withholding any part of the deposit.

Other charges

Cleaning and rent arrears are charged in full — fair wear & tear and betterment apply only to damaged or missing items, not to cleanliness or unpaid rent.

Apportionment follows the standard fair-wear-and-tear formula — cost quoted × remaining life ÷ expected life — and is a working estimate only, not a scheme ruling: real adjudications also weigh check-in/check-out photos, the item's actual condition and how the tenancy was used. This tool assumes years for item life and age; convert months to a decimal (e.g. 18 months = 1.5 years) before entering.
