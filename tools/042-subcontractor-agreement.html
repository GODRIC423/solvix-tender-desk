Not legal advice. This tool assembles a plain-English sub-contract from the details you enter and calculates the payment figures that follow from them. It is a drafting aid only: it is not a solicitor, it does not know your main contract, and it does not check that these terms are enforceable in your situation. Payment terms are drafted to reflect Part II of the Housing Grants, Construction and Regeneration Act 1996 (as amended), but nothing here overrides that Act, the Scheme for Construction Contracts, or a main contract you are bound by. Have the finished document checked by a construction solicitor before either party signs, and confirm CIS status and VAT treatment with your accountant or HMRC. Figures are estimates for the schedule of payments and are not a tax calculation or a certificate.

Payment provisions follow Part II of the Housing Grants, Construction and Regeneration Act 1996 as amended by the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009 (due dates, payment and pay less notices, suspension and adjudication); the Scheme for Construction Contracts applies where this agreement is silent. CIS deductions follow the Construction Industry Scheme (Finance Act 2004 and the Income Tax (Construction Industry Scheme) Regulations 2005) — deducted from the labour element only, at 0%, 20% or 30%. The VAT domestic reverse charge for building and construction services has applied since 1 March 2021. Employers' liability cover of at least £5,000,000 is required by the Employers' Liability (Compulsory Insurance) Act 1969. Health and safety duties reference the Construction (Design and Management) Regulations 2015. Figures exclude interest, contra charges and any main contract deductions. Not legal or tax advice.
