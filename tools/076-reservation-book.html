What this tool assumes. Cover capacity for a service = Σ over tables in service of (seats × floor(service minutes ÷ turn time for a party of that table’s size)); the walk-in hold is that capacity × your percentage, rounded up; bookable covers = capacity − hold; remaining covers = bookable − covers booked (statuses booked, confirmed and seated). A table is occupied from the booking time until the turn time for that party size has elapsed, then released. No-show rate = no-shows ÷ bookings excluding cancellations. Times are venue local time; a service closing before it opens is treated as running past midnight. Everything is stored in this browser only — nothing is sent anywhere.
