Estimate only — check before you dig. This tool counts materials from the dimensions you type in. It does not check your boundary line, planning or deed restrictions, wind loading, ground conditions, or the position of buried gas, water, electricity and telecoms services. Confirm the boundary with your neighbour or title plan, have services located before excavating, and take advice from your supplier, a structural engineer or your local building authority for anything over 2 m high, retaining ground, or in an exposed position.
— Materials always rounded up
Method. Per run: posts = ceil(fenced run ÷ post spacing) + 1, plus one post per corner and two posts per gate; bays (panels) = posts − 1 for the straight run; rails = bays × rails per bay; pickets = ceil(fenced run ÷ (picket width + gap)). Fenced run = run length − (gates × gate opening width). Post hole volume = π r² × depth, and bags = ceil(total volume ÷ bag yield). Default embedment is the one-third-of-fence-height rule of thumb. Bag yields are the manufacturers' nominal figures — 80 lb 0.60 ft³, 60 lb 0.45 ft³, 50 lb 0.375 ft³, 40 lb 0.30 ft³, 25 kg 0.0125 m³, 20 kg post mix 0.010 m³ — and vary with water content and compaction. Conversions: 1 ft = 0.3048 m, 1 in = 25.4 mm, 1 ft³ = 0.028316846592 m³. Quantities are rounded up; money is rounded to two decimal places. An estimating aid only — not a structural design, and not a substitute for your supplier's, engineer's or building authority's advice.
