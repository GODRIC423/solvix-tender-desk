Gross profit per unit —
Margin = (price − cost) ÷ price. Markup = (price − cost) ÷ cost — the two standard, universally used definitions, which is exactly why they're constantly mixed up: the same 25 percentage points means very different money depending which one you're quoting. Multi-tier margins are measured against the one unit cost, never against the tier below. The discount-impact volume assumes unit cost is unchanged and that the extra units sold carry the same margin as today. All money to 2 decimal places. This is a pricing calculator, not accounting or tax advice — agree final prices with whoever owns your P&L. Everything is stored only in this browser.
