VAT treatment
What this tool does and does not do. It costs recipes from the purchase prices, pack sizes and yields you enter, and classifies dishes on the sales figures you enter. It is a management aid, not accounting, tax or nutrition advice. It does not include labour, energy, packaging, overheads, till discounts, wastage beyond the yields entered, or service charge. VAT rates and the liability of individual food items are for you to confirm — check with your accountant or tax authority before you price from this.

Popularity threshold is the standard 70% rule: (100% ÷ number of dishes) × 0.70. The margin threshold is the weighted average contribution margin of the dishes analysed.

Plate cost = Σ (recipe quantity × EP unit cost), where EP unit cost = pack price ÷ pack size ÷ yield %; divided by the portions the recipe yields. Food cost % and gross profit are calculated on the net (VAT-exclusive) selling price, the standard hospitality basis. Menu engineering follows Kasavana & Smith (1982): menu mix versus contribution margin, 70% popularity rule, weighted average contribution margin. Imperial volumes are US measure (1 US fl oz = 29.5735 ml). Money is rounded to 2 decimal places for display; all arithmetic is carried at full precision.
