Method. Total miles = loaded + deadhead. Revenue = flat rate, or rate per loaded mile × loaded miles. Fuel = total miles ÷ fuel economy × fuel price (metric: total km × L/100 km ÷ 100 × price per litre). Driver pay = loaded miles × cents per mile, or percentage of revenue, or hours × hourly rate. Fixed cost = total miles × your fixed cost per mile. Net = revenue − fuel − driver − fixed − tolls − accessorials. Margin = net ÷ revenue. Break-even rate = the revenue that makes net zero, divided by loaded miles (with percentage pay, break-even revenue = other costs ÷ (1 − pay %)). Verdict: a loss is Decline, a positive net below your target margin is Marginal, at or above target is Take it. Conversions: 1 mile = 1.609344 km, 1 US gallon = 3.785411784 L, L/100 km = 235.214583 ÷ MPG. Money is rounded to 2 decimal places for display; all arithmetic uses full precision.
