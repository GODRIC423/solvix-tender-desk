A planning worksheet — not a contract, and not a quote. Every figure here is one you typed: the tool adds up your own estimates, quotes, deposits and final costs and tells you what is left, what is due next and how many people you are feeding. It does not check a supplier's price, hold a booking, take a payment, send an invitation or chase an RSVP, and it does not know a venue's cancellation terms. Confirm every price, deposit, final-numbers deadline and cancellation clause in writing with the supplier before you rely on it. The guest list holds names, dietary and contact-style details in this browser only — nothing is uploaded — so treat printed lists and exported files as personal data belonging to your guests.
