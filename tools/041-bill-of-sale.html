    What this tool does: it assembles a bill of sale from the details you enter and computes the consideration as subtotal − trade-in allowance = taxable consideration, plus tax at the rate you set, plus any documentation fee, giving the total sale price (also written out in words); the deposit paid is deducted to give the balance due. Money is rounded to 2 decimal places at each line. Odometer wording follows the three standard federal disclosure statements used under 49 CFR Part 580 (actual mileage / exceeds mechanical limits / not the actual mileage), with an exempt option; unit conversion uses 1 mile = 1.609344 km exactly. What it does not do: it does not give legal advice, check your jurisdiction's statutes, verify title or liens, or calculate the tax your authority will actually charge on transfer. Everything stays on this device — nothing is uploaded. Consult a qualified lawyer and your licensing or registration authority before relying on this document.
