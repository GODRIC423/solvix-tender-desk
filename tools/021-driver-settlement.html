    Not a tax document. This worksheet calculates settlement pay from the figures you enter. It does not compute income tax, national insurance, FICA/PAYE withholding, workers’ compensation or any statutory deduction, and it is not a W-2, 1099, P60 or payslip for filing purposes. Detention, stop and accessorial pay must match your driver contract or collective agreement; escrow handling is regulated for leased owner-operators (in the USA see 49 CFR §376.12(k)). Check every figure against the settlement your carrier issues and consult your accountant or payroll provider before relying on these numbers.
