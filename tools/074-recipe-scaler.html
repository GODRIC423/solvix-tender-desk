Baker's percentages are expressed against total flour weight (flour = 100%), the standard bakery formula convention. Mass conversions use 1 oz = 28.349523125 g and 1 lb = 453.59237 g; volume conversions follow the spoon & cup standard you select. Ingredients entered by volume are counted toward batch weight and baker's percentages at 1.00 g/ml — weigh them for exact formula work. Ingredients counted by number are excluded from batch weight. Edible-portion cost = as-purchased cost ÷ yield %. Allergen lists follow Regulation (EU) No 1169/2011 Annex II (14 allergens, retained in UK law) or the US FALCPA as amended by the FASTER Act 2021 (9 major allergens).
