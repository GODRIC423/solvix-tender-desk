What this tool assumes. Net area per room = length × width less the cut-outs you list. Waste allowances follow common trade practice: straight / stack-bond 10%, diagonal (45°) 15%, herringbone 20%. Boxes = ⌈ area × (1 + waste) ÷ coverage per box ⌉, always rounded up; the headline figure rounds up once across the whole job, the per-room column rounds up each room separately. Grout uses the published trade approximation (tile length + tile width) ÷ (tile length × tile width) × joint width × tile thickness × area × 1.5 = lb, worked in inches and square feet and converted for display — it is an approximation only, and grout, adhesive and underlay coverage all vary with trowel size, substrate flatness and installer technique. Adhesive, underlay and labour are figured on the net floor area. Nothing here is a substitute for the manufacturer's data sheet or a site survey. All data stays in this browser — nothing is uploaded.
