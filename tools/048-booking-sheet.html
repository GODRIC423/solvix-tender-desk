A booking sheet, not a compliance or payment record. This tool prevents double-booking on the same person, room, chair or bay and reports how full the day is. It does not check staff working-time limits, rest breaks, ratios, insurance or licensing, it does not take payment or hold a deposit, and it does not send reminders. It holds client names and contact details in this browser only — nothing is uploaded — so treat printed sheets and exported files as personal data and handle them under your own data-protection duties. Confirm anything contractual (cancellation terms, deposits, staff hours) with the person responsible for it.
