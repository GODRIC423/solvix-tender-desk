Check against your adopted building code before you cut. This tool applies the common residential stair limits (riser 4 in–7¾ in, tread depth ≥ 10 in, riser + tread 17–18 in, 2 × riser + tread 24–25 in, headroom ≥ 6 ft 8 in) to the figures you type. It does not know your jurisdiction's amendments, occupancy class, guard and handrail rules, landing sizes or structural sizing, and it does not replace a plans examiner, building control officer, architect or structural engineer. Measure the finished floor-to-finished-floor rise on site, and verify every figure before ordering or cutting material.

Produced with the Solvix Stair Rise & Run Calculator. Figures derived from the input dimensions shown on this document. All dimensions to be verified on site against the finished floor levels and against the building code adopted by the authority having jurisdiction before any material is ordered or cut.
Assumes a straight flight of uniform risers, total rise measured finished floor to finished floor, tread depth measured nosing to nosing (the going), and stringers cut from a single length. Limits applied: riser 4 in–7.75 in, tread depth ≥ 10 in, riser + tread 17–18 in, 2 × riser + tread 24–25 in, headroom ≥ 6 ft 8 in (2032 mm) — the residential values in IRC R311.7 and the classic Blondel rule. Metric equivalents are converted from those inch values. Everything is stored in this browser only; nothing is uploaded.
