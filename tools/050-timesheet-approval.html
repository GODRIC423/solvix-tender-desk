Rules applied—
Worked hours = clock-out − clock-in − unpaid break, treating a shift that ends before it starts as overnight. Overtime is the excess over each employee's contracted weekly hours (or a fixed weekly threshold if selected) and is costed at the multiplier shown; basic and overtime pay are each rounded to 2 decimal places before totalling, and department totals are the sum of those rounded timesheet costs. Exception thresholds (contracted hours, expected days, minimum shift, break required after, minimum break) are user-set house rules. Gross cost only — no PAYE, NI, pension, holiday pay or Working Time Regulations assessment. Data stays in this browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
