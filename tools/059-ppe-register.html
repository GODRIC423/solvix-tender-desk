PPE Issue Register

ISSUE LOG · REPLACEMENT DUE · COST TRACKING
Important notice. This is a record of PPE issued, as entered by whoever maintains this register. It is not a PPE risk assessment. Replacement dates are calculated from the expected-life figure set for each item and flag when equipment is due for a routine swap — they do not know whether a specific item has been damaged, contaminated, lost its fit, or is otherwise unsafe to keep using; any PPE found unfit for use must be replaced immediately regardless of this schedule. Deciding what PPE a role needs, and reviewing that decision, is a duty on the employer under the Personal Protective Equipment at Work Regulations 1992 (as amended 2022) and must be carried out by a competent person — this tool only checks your entries against the requirements you have told it to expect.
This is a record-keeping tool, not a PPE risk assessment. It stores what you enter and flags equipment that is overdue for its scheduled replacement, missing against the role requirements you define, or not returned by a leaver. It cannot judge whether PPE is actually fit for use, or whether a role's requirements are correct.
What you must still do
Decide what PPE each role needs, and review that decision, under the Personal Protective Equipment at Work Regulations 1992 (as amended 2022) — this is a duty on the employer and must be carried out by a competent person. Replace any item immediately if it is damaged, contaminated or no longer fits, whatever this register's due date says. This register only tracks the data you put into it.

PPE Issue Register. Next-due dates are calculated as issue date plus the expected-life figure set for each catalogue item (in whole calendar months); "due soon" applies the warning window set above, "overdue" means the date has already passed and no replacement has been issued. Only active employees' equipment counts toward the replacement-due totals and the missing-PPE check; a departed employee's un-returned kit appears separately under leavers. Cost figures total each issue's quantity × the catalogue item's unit cost at the time you view the register (not a historical price record) and are shown to 2 decimal places. All data stays in this browser; nothing is uploaded anywhere.
