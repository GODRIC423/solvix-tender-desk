Record-keeping aid — not proof of competence. This matrix reports only the dates you enter and the validity periods you set against each qualification. It does not verify certificates with the awarding body, does not assess whether anyone is competent to do a task, and does not decide your legal duties. Check the original certificate or card, confirm the renewal period with the awarding organisation, and take advice from a competent health and safety adviser before relying on this document for site access, tender submissions, insurance or enforcement.

Compliance by qualification

Expiry = achieved date + the qualification's validity in calendar months (day-of-month clamped to the month end, so 29 Feb + 36 months = 28 Feb). Qualifications set to “end of month” expire on the last day of that month. A certificate counts as valid up to and including its expiry date; it is amber from the window start (default 60 days) and red the day after expiry. Compliance % = required qualifications currently valid ÷ required qualifications, by role. Certificates held but not required by the role are recorded and marked “extra” but are excluded from the compliance percentage. Everything stays in this browser — no data is sent anywhere.
