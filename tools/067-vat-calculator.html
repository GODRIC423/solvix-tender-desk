This tool does the arithmetic. It does not decide your tax position. It applies the rates and treatments you choose to the figures you enter. It does not know whether a supply is standard-rated, reduced-rated, zero-rated, exempt, subject to the reverse charge or outside the scope of tax, whether you are registered, which country's rules apply, or when a place-of-supply or margin scheme changes the answer. Preset rates are a convenience, are editable, and change without notice. Check the current rate and the correct treatment with the tax authority or your accountant before you issue, file or pay anything on the strength of these figures.

Zero-rated is taxable at 0% and counts in taxable turnover. Exempt carries no tax and is not taxable turnover. Reverse charge lines carry no tax on this document — the customer accounts for it — but stay in the document total, and the tax they must account for is shown as a memorandum. Total to collect —
Adds tax as net × (1 + rate) and removes it as gross ÷ (1 + rate) — never by subtracting the rate from the gross. Each tax code is subtotalled separately as a return requires; zero-rated and exempt are kept apart; reverse-charge and out-of-scope lines are excluded from the tax total but stay in the document total. Money is rounded half-up to 2 decimal places. Preset rates are editable defaults, not tax advice — check the current rate and treatment with the tax authority.
