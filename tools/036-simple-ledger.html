Not statutory accounts and not audited. No VAT, payroll tax, corporation tax, depreciation or accrual has been calculated. Consult a qualified accountant before filing or relying on these figures. Produced with the Solvix Double-Entry Ledger.
Double entry throughout: every entry must have total debits equal to total credits before it posts, so the trial balance can only differ if unposted drafts are included. Trial balance and balance sheet are cumulative to the period end date; profit & loss covers the period dates only. Assets and expenses carry debit balances; liabilities, equity and income carry credit balances. Money is held to the exact penny (integer minor units) and shown to 2 dp; negatives are shown in brackets. No framework (UK GAAP, FRS 102 or IFRS), tax or audit treatment is applied — this is a bookkeeping aid, not statutory accounts. Everything is stored in this browser only.
