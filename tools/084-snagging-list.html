This is a defect record, not a survey or an inspection report. It organises the snags you enter and does the arithmetic on them. It does not inspect the building, does not decide whether an item is a defect or an acceptable tolerance, and does not determine who is contractually liable — that depends on your contract and specification. Anything structural, electrical, gas or fire-related must be assessed by a qualified person, and anything unsafe reported at once rather than added to a list. Sign-off recorded here is your own record of acceptance; it is not practical completion, a warranty inspection or a statutory certificate.

Snagging & Defects List. Completion is measured against the live list: snags marked rejected are taken out of the denominator (they are not accepted as defects), so completion % = closed ÷ (total − rejected) × 100, shown to one decimal place and to “—” when there is nothing live to measure. The priority-weighted figure uses the same formula with each snag weighted urgent 8, high 4, medium 2, low 1, so closing cosmetic items cannot mask outstanding urgent ones. A snag is overdue when its target date has passed and it is neither closed nor rejected; days are whole calendar days from local midnight. Ages are counted from the date raised. Money is totalled to two decimal places in the currency you choose and is an estimate you enter, not a valuation. Everything is stored in this browser only — nothing is uploaded, and clearing your browser data deletes it.
