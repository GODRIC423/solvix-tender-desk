Not legal advice — employment law is jurisdiction-specific. This tool assembles an offer letter from the details you type and costs the package that follows. It is a drafting aid: it does not know the law of the country, state or province where this person will work, and it cannot confirm that any term here is lawful or enforceable for you. Statutory figures shown for reference (holiday minimums, pension earnings bands, notice periods) are editable defaults you must verify against the rules in force for your jurisdiction. Have this letter, and the contract of employment that must follow it, reviewed by a qualified employment lawyer before you send it — and confirm tax, payroll and right-to-work treatment with your accountant and the relevant authority.

A discretionary bonus with no stated target is valued at nil in the package total — only contractual or on-target amounts are counted.

Pension / retirement plan

Defaults shown are the UK automatic-enrolment qualifying earnings band (£6,240–£50,270) — confirm the figures in force for the current tax year, or switch the basis to full salary / a 401(k)-style match on pay.

Holiday / paid time off

Day and month are used; the year is ignored.

Probation & notice

Statutory minimum notice can rise above what you set here as service builds up (in the UK, one week per complete year of service after two years, to a maximum of twelve). Check the rule that applies in your jurisdiction — the contract cannot go below it.
