This is a stock-check tool, not a certificate of compliance. The pre-loaded contents lists follow the item ranges commonly published for BS 8599-1 workplace kits (small, medium, large, travel) and BS 8599-2 vehicle kits. They are supplied as an editable starting point and are not a substitute for the published standard, which you should check yourself. In Great Britain the Health and Safety (First-Aid) Regulations 1981 require kit contents to be decided by your own first-aid needs assessment — there is no legally fixed list, and a BS 8599 kit is not mandatory. This tool only compares the numbers and dates you type in: it cannot tell whether sterile packaging is intact, whether an item is fit for use, or whether you have enough trained first aiders and appointed persons. Confirm quantities against your current risk assessment and take advice from a competent occupational health and safety adviser.

Recording an audit stamps the kit's last-audit date and recalculates when the next check falls due.
Required quantities are pre-loaded from the item ranges commonly published for BS 8599-1 workplace kits (small / medium / large / travel) and BS 8599-2 vehicle kits, and are fully editable — the standard's own tables take precedence over anything shown here. Shortfall = required − quantity found; an item past its expiry date counts as zero usable stock, so its restock quantity is the full required amount. Restock quantities are whole units, rounded up. Next audit due = last audit date + audit interval in calendar months. Everything is stored only in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
