Load per operative
Planning estimate only. Projected arrival times are worked from the shift start plus the travel and on-site minutes you enter. They take no account of traffic, vehicle breakdowns, parking or job overruns. Committed-minute totals are not a check on drivers' hours, working-time limits or rest-break law — confirm those against the rules that apply to your operation and your own contracts of employment.
Saved to this browser

Each operative's run sheet starts on a new page, with blank arrive / leave / signature boxes so it can be worked on paper and handed back in.

Available minutes = (shift end − shift start) − break. Committed minutes = Σ (on-site duration + travel) for every job assigned to that operative, whatever its status. Utilisation = committed ÷ available. Projected arrival runs jobs in board order from the shift start, waiting where you arrive before the window opens, and inserting the unpaid break before the first job that would start after the shift midpoint. A job is flagged at risk when projected arrival is later than the end of its time window. All times are local clock times, 24-hour. Data is held only in this browser — nothing is sent anywhere.
