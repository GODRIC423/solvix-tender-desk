Condition is graded New / Good / Fair / Poor / Damaged and cleanliness Professionally cleaned / Clean / Domestic clean / Marked / Dirty, each scored 5 to 1. An item is flagged when the check-out grade is below the check-in grade on either scale. Replacement charges are apportioned straight-line over the useful lifespan entered (the betterment principle applied by tenancy deposit adjudicators); repairs and cleaning are charged at cost. Lifespan figures in the picker are typical guide values published by deposit protection schemes and are not a statutory table. Money is rounded to 2 decimal places.
