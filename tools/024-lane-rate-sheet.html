A commercial comparison worksheet — not a rate confirmation or a contract of carriage. The totals are only as good as the quotes typed in: confirm in writing what each price includes (fuel, detention, stop-offs, layover, driver assist, lumper, tarps, tolls, permits, reconsignment), which accessorial tariff applies, whether the mileage basis is practical or shortest, and how long the quote stands. This sheet does not check operating authority, insurance, safety rating or cargo liability — verify FMCSA authority (MC/USDOT), a current certificate of insurance and your own broker or shipper contract terms before tendering the load.

Fuel surcharge helper

standard peg formula

The usual truckload formula: FSC per mile = (average diesel price − peg price) ÷ miles per gallon, floored at zero. Take the diesel price from the current EIA/DOE weekly on-highway diesel average for your region; the peg and the MPG assumption come from your carrier agreement.
