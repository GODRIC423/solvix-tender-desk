Not legal or payroll advice. Tip-pooling rules — who may be included (front vs back of house, supervisors, owners), how tips interact with minimum wage or a "tip credit", and record-keeping duties — vary by country, state/province and sometimes by city, and change over time. This tool only does the arithmetic you ask it to. Confirm your pooling policy with your labour lawyer, accountant or local wage authority before you pay out.

#NameRole HoursPoints ShareCash share Card shareTotal (exact) Payout (rounded)
Points = hours × role weight (equal split uses weight 1 and ignores hours in the weighting). Share = points ÷ total points × pool. Cash and card are split in the same proportion, then shown separately.

Pool = cash tips + (card tips × (1 − processing fee %)) when the fee deduction is on. Per person: points = hours worked × role weight; share = points ÷ total points of everyone who worked that shift × pool. Cash and card shares use the same share fraction so the two funding sources stay traceable. Payouts round to the nearest note/coin denomination you choose; the rounding remainder — the gap between the exact pool and the sum of rounded payouts — is shown on every table so nothing goes missing silently. Week figures sum each person's exact share across every shift entered, then round once at the week total. Money to 2 decimal places; nothing here is uploaded, everything is stored in this browser only.
