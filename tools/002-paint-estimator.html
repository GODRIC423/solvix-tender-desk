Volumes are the paint actually needed. Cans are bought for the job as a whole, not per room, so the job total is rounded up once per product.

Basis of this estimate

    How it calculates. Wall area = perimeter (2 × [L + W]) × height, less the openings you count (default 21 ft² per door, 15 ft² per window). Ceiling = L × W. Volume = area × coats ÷ coverage rate. Cans = ⌈ volume ÷ can size ⌉, rounded up per product across the whole job. Primer is a separate pass over the walls, plus the ceiling where the ceiling is painted. Default coverage 350 ft² per US gallon (8.6 m² per litre) — a typical spread rate for interior wall paint on prepared surfaces; always use the figure on your own product's data sheet.
