How this works. Every month, the minimum payment is made on every debt. Your extra payment — plus the minimum payments freed up from debts already paid off — is rolled onto one target debt: the smallest balance (Snowball) or the highest interest rate (Avalanche). Assumes a fixed APR, no new charges, and payments made on schedule every month.
