What a signed delivery note does and does not prove. A signature records that the stated number of packages was handed over at that address, date and time — not that the contents were counted, tested or accepted, and not who pays for a shortage or for damage. Your contract of sale and the carrier's terms decide that. This note is not a customs document and not a dangerous-goods declaration: the handling flags are instructions to the driver only. Write any shortage or damage on the note before signing and tell the carrier inside their claim window. Cross-border, ADR/IATA/IMDG or temperature-controlled loads need the proper paperwork from your carrier or freight forwarder.

Back-ordered = max(ordered − shipped, 0) per line. Gross weight = Σ(package qty × weight each). Volumetric weight = Σ(qty × L × W × H) ÷ divisor (5000 is the common IATA air-freight divisor for cm/kg; 139 the usual in/lb equivalent) — confirm the divisor and the rounding your own carrier applies. Chargeable weight = the greater of gross and volumetric. Values, where shown, exclude tax and carriage. Everything is stored in this browser only; nothing is sent anywhere.
