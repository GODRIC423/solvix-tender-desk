Not legal advice. This tool assembles a plain-English non-disclosure agreement from the details you enter and works out the dates that follow from your term and survival period. It is a drafting aid only. It is not a solicitor or attorney, it has not read your other contracts, it does not know your industry's regulatory duties, and it cannot confirm that these terms are enforceable where you are — the enforceability of confidentiality, non-solicitation and long survival periods varies by jurisdiction and by the facts. Have this document reviewed by a qualified lawyer in the governing law you have selected before either party signs it, and take your own advice on personal data, export-controlled or regulated information, which are not covered here.

Draft agreement

—
Assumes a business-to-business exchange of commercial information between two parties. Dates are calendar dates from the effective date, clamped to the end of a short month (e.g. 31 Jan + 1 month = 28/29 Feb). Structure follows ordinary commercial NDA practice; third-party-rights wording follows the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 1999 for England, Wales and Northern Ireland and the Contract (Third Party Rights) (Scotland) Act 2017 for Scotland; the optional whistleblower notice is the immunity notice at 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b) (Defend Trade Secrets Act 2016). Nothing here is legal advice. Data stays in this browser — nothing is uploaded.
