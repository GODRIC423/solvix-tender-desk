STATUTORY INTEREST · ESCALATING DEMAND LETTERS
Read before you send this. This tool drafts a demand for payment and calculates interest arithmetic — it is not legal advice and it does not itself preserve or create any legal right. Statutory interest and fixed compensation under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998 apply only to qualifying business-to-business contracts, and the base rate you must use is the Bank of England rate in force on the 31 December or 30 June before the debt became late — check it and enter it below. If the debtor is an individual or a sole trader, the Pre-Action Protocol for Debt Claims applies and requires a compliant Letter of Claim with an information sheet, reply form and 30 days to respond; this letter is not that document. Never threaten action you are not prepared to take. Check every figure, and take advice from a solicitor or your accountant before starting recovery proceedings.
