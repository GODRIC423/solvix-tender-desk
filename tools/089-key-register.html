Key & Access Register

ISSUE · OUTSTANDING · LOST-KEY · AUDIT
This register is only as accurate as what is entered. It tracks who a key was issued to and computes days held, overdue and lost status from the dates you type in — it does not verify physical possession, does not detect unauthorised copies, and is not a substitute for a periodic physical key audit. Where a master key or a key to a controlled or high-security area is lost, treat the affected locks as compromised and get a locksmith's assessment before relying on this record alone.
