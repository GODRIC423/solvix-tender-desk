Basis of the estimate

What this tool assumes. Wall area = 2 × (length + width) × wall height; ceiling area = length × width where ticked; openings are deducted at the nominal areas you set. Sheets = area × (1 + waste) ÷ sheet area, rounded up room by room and summed. Screws at 32 per sheet. Joint tape = 1.4 × (sheets × sheet perimeter ÷ 2), the shared-edge joint length. Joint compound at 4.5 US gal per 1000 ft² of board for a three-coat finish (≈ 0.053 lb per ft² of lightweight all-purpose mud). Corner bead = external corner count × wall height. Sheet sizes 4×8 ft = 32 ft², 4×10 ft = 40 ft², 4×12 ft = 48 ft².

What it is not. Not a fire-, acoustic- or moisture-rated specification and not a substitute for the drawings, the board manufacturer's published coverage data, or your local building code. Rates are trade rules of thumb: check them against the products you actually buy. Nothing leaves this page — it runs entirely offline and stores your take-off in this browser only.
