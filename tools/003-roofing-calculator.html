Estimate only — check it on the roof. This take-off is a material estimate from the footprint and pitch you entered. It is not a structural, ventilation, fire or building-code assessment, and it does not replace the manufacturer’s written installation instructions, which govern the warranty. Verify every dimension on site, confirm coverage figures against the products actually being supplied, and check ice-barrier, ventilation and deck requirements with your local building control or code official. What this tool assumes. Footprint dimensions are horizontal plan measurements; the pitch multiplier √(1 + (rise ÷ 12)²) converts them to true sloped area. One roofing square = 100 sq ft (9.29 m²). Field shingles at 3 bundles per square (3-tab and most architectural laminates), underlayment at 4 squares per roll for 15 lb felt, 2 for 30 lb and 10 for synthetic, fasteners at 320 nails per square, waste at 10% simple / 15% cut-up. Underlayment, drip edge, starter, cap and membrane are taken off measured runs without a waste uplift; field shingles and nails carry the waste allowance. Every pack quantity is rounded up to a whole unit. Coverage rates vary by manufacturer — overwrite them with the figures on the product you are actually buying. Nothing leaves this browser; the tool works with no network at all.
