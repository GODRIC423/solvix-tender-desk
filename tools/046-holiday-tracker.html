This tool does the arithmetic you tell it to: it pro-rates the entitlement you enter by calendar days in post and by contracted days per week, subtracts the leave you record, and flags dates where more of a team are off than the limit you set. It is not a statutory entitlement calculator, it does not know your jurisdiction, it does not apply reference-period averaging, irregular-hours or term-time rules, and it does not decide whether leave can be carried over or must be paid in lieu. Public holidays are only the dates in your own list. For statutory minimums, contractual terms or a dispute, take advice from a qualified employment adviser.

Leave year = the start date you set, running to the day before the same date one year later. Entitlement per employee = full-time annual days × (contracted days per week ÷ working days per week) × (calendar days in post inside the leave year ÷ calendar days in the leave year), rounded up to the next half day so a part-year or part-time figure is never rounded down. Allowance = entitlement + brought-forward days, with brought-forward capped at the carry-over limit. A booking's days = every date from the start to the end inclusive that is both a ticked working day of the week and not on the public holiday list; a booking marked as a half day counts 0.5 and only where it covers a single working day. Only bookings inside the leave year count against it. Remaining = allowance − approved − pending annual leave. A clash day is any date on which more people in the same team are absent than the limit you set. Individual part-time working patterns (which specific weekdays a part-timer works) are not modelled. Data is saved only in this browser; nothing is sent anywhere. Not employment, payroll or legal advice.
