Illustration only — this is not a credit quotation. It assumes every payment is made in full and on time and that the rate never changes, and it excludes fees, charges, insurance, early-repayment penalties and day-count adjustments. Your lender's figures are the ones that count. Check this against your credit agreement and take advice from a qualified accountant, broker or regulated financial adviser. Interest here is charged on the balance at each payment date, not daily, and each period's interest is rounded to the nearest minor unit. Real agreements may use daily accrual, different day-count conventions and their own rounding, so a lender's figures can differ by a few units per payment. No tax treatment is modelled.

Method. Payment by the standard annuity (amortisation) formula P = r·PV ⁄ (1 − (1 + r)−n), where PV is the amount advanced, n the number of payments and r the periodic interest rate. With a balloon B the formula becomes P = r·(PV − B·(1 + r)−n) ⁄ (1 − (1 + r)−n); with an interest-only period the amortising formula is applied over the payments that remain after it. Periodic rate: nominal basis r = i ⁄ f; effective-annual basis r = (1 + i)1/f − 1, where f is payments per year (12, 26 or 52). Each period, interest = opening balance × r rounded to the cent, principal = payment − interest, closing = opening − principal; the final payment is adjusted to clear the balance exactly and includes any balloon. Interest is calculated on the balance each period, not daily; no fees, charges or rate changes are modelled. This tool gives arithmetic, not financial advice, and is not a regulated credit illustration.
