This is a record form, not a risk assessment, a method statement or a training certificate. A toolbox talk is a short reminder of hazards and agreed controls on a job that has already been risk assessed. It does not replace the risk assessment, the method statement or the permit, and attending a talk does not make anyone trained, competent or authorised for a task. The talking points, hazards and controls supplied in the built-in library are generic prompts written for common construction work in Great Britain: they are a starting point that you must check, edit and add to so they describe the actual work, plant, substances and site conditions on the day. Legal references are given for orientation only and are not legal advice; duties differ outside Great Britain. This tool records what you type — it cannot confirm that anyone understood the briefing, that a control is adequate, or that a signature is genuine. Keep the signed sheet with your health and safety file, and take advice from a competent health and safety adviser where you are unsure.
