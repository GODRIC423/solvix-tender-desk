      What this tool does. It records an incident and applies the published reportability tests in RIDDOR 2013 (SI 2013/1471) — reg. 4 non-fatal injuries to workers including the specified-injury list and over-seven-day incapacitation, reg. 5 injuries to non-workers, reg. 6 work-related fatalities, reg. 7 and schedule 2 dangerous occurrences, reg. 8 occupational diseases and reg. 12 records — and in OSHA 29 CFR 1904 — 1904.4–1904.11 recording criteria, 1904.7 day counts and 300-log columns, and 1904.39 severe-injury reporting. Incapacity is counted in consecutive calendar days from the day after the incident, including weekends and rest days; OSHA day counts are capped at 180. Money is shown to 2 decimal places. Everything is stored in this browser only. It is not legal advice and it does not submit anything to any authority.
