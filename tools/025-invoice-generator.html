It does not decide whether a rate is right for your goods, your place of supply or your registration status, it does not handle reverse-charge, margin or exemption rules, and it is not a book of account. Confirm the treatment with your accountant or tax authority before you send, and keep your own statutory records.

Totals —— —
Line net = quantity × unit price − line discount (a percentage of that line, or a fixed amount capped at it), each rounded half-up to 2 decimal places. Tax is totalled per rate band on the sum of the rounded line nets — shipping joins the band matching its own rate — so the printed tax summary always reconciles. Total = net + shipping + tax. Balance due = total − deposit received. Due date = invoice date + the days in your terms. Money is shown to 2 dp in the number format you pick; quantities to 3 dp. Everything is stored in this browser only — nothing is sent anywhere.
