Planning estimate — not a certified load plan and not a carrier quotation. Internal dimensions and payload vary from box to box, and road, rail or terminal weight limits may cut the payload further. The carton fit is plain geometry: no allowance for pallets, dunnage, lashing, door clearance, stack strength, weight distribution or cargo that cannot be turned. Under SOLAS Chapter VI the verified gross mass (VGM) must be established by weighing, never calculated from this sheet. Check the container's CSC plate and confirm chargeable weight with your carrier or forwarder before booking.

Saved in this browser only

The stow factor is a planning allowance for the space you never get to use — pallet overhang, dunnage, uneven layers, cargo that cannot be turned. It affects only the "at stow factor" column of the container comparison; every other figure on this sheet is a true 100% calculation.

LCL rate (optional)

Ocean LCL is normally rated per revenue ton (W/M): the greater of cubic metres and metric tonnes, one revenue ton per whole or part unit as your carrier's tariff states. This estimate covers the freight line only — not THC, documentation, customs, CFS or destination charges.

CBM per carton = length × width × height in metres. Line CBM = CBM per carton × cartons. Volumes are shown to 4 decimal places per carton and 3 for totals; carton counts are whole cartons, rounded down to what physically fits.

CBM = L × W × H in metres per carton × quantity. LCL chargeable weight follows the ocean freight W/M rule — the greater of total cubic metres and total weight in metric tonnes (1 m³ = 1,000 kg). Carton fit is a plain three-axis calculation, floor(container L ÷ carton L) × floor(container W ÷ carton W) × floor(container H ÷ carton H), taking the best of the six axis permutations. Container capacities are nominal industry values for standard dry boxes and vary between builds; verify against the CSC plate on the actual container. Works offline; all data stays in this browser.
