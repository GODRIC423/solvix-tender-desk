How this is worked out.
Indicative estimate — not a payroll or billing figure. It costs the time of the people you list, using the rates you type and the on-cost percentage you set. It is not a chargeable rate, it does not include room, travel, software or preparation time, and it must not be used for invoicing, pay decisions or any employment action. Check pay figures with your payroll or finance team before sharing them, and treat individual salary data as confidential.
Meeting cost —
Salary is converted to an hourly rate by dividing by the working hours per year you set (default 1,880 h); every rate is then multiplied by (1 + employer on-costs %). Meeting cost = total loaded hourly rate × minutes ÷ 60. Annual cost = cost per occurrence × occurrences per year. Figures are estimates from your own inputs and are rounded to two decimal places for display only. Everything stays in this browser — nothing is sent anywhere.
