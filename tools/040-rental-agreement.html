This is a drafting aid, not legal advice. It assembles a rental agreement from your answers and works out the rent, deposit and dates that follow. It is not a solicitor or attorney, it has not read your circumstances, and it cannot confirm these terms are lawful, complete or enforceable where you are. Residential tenancies are heavily regulated. Deposit protection and caps, prescribed information, notice periods, eviction procedure, licensing, gas and energy safety, right-to-rent checks, prohibited fees and repairing obligations differ by country, state and city, change often, and local law overrides anything printed here — a term that conflicts with it is void. Have a qualified lawyer in the relevant jurisdiction review and adapt this document before either party signs, and confirm tax treatment with your accountant.
