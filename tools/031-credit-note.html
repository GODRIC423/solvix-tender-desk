Not tax or legal advice. This tool builds a credit note document and reverses tax arithmetically at the rates you enter on the original lines. It does not verify tax registration numbers, decide whether a credit note is permitted or required in your jurisdiction, apply cash-accounting or margin schemes, handle foreign-exchange restatement, or file anything with a tax authority. Numbering is stored on this device only. Check the finished document against your own records and your accountant's advice before you send it.
Line net = credited quantity × original unit price (or the fixed amount you enter, capped at the line's net). Tax is reversed proportionally at each line's original rate, totalled per rate band on the rounded net credited and rounded half-up to 2 decimal places, so the printed tax summary always reconciles to the total. Remaining balance = original invoice total including tax − credit notes already issued against it − this note. Everything is stored in this browser only; nothing is sent anywhere.
