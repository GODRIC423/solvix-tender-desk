Read before you sign. This tool records and prints the driver’s own walkaround inspection. It does not inspect the vehicle, does not certify roadworthiness and is not a legal compliance ruling. Whether a defect puts a vehicle out of service is decided by the CVSA North American Standard Out-of-Service Criteria (or the DVSA categorisation of defects in the UK) together with your operator’s own policy — not by this form. Repairs must be carried out and certified by a qualified mechanic. If you are in any doubt about a defect, do not drive the vehicle and speak to your transport or safety manager.

Check groups follow the driver vehicle inspection report required by 49 CFR §396.11 and the pre-trip check of §396.13, expanded with the vehicle systems listed in Appendix G to Subchapter B (exhaust, fuel system, frame, suspension) and the cargo securement checks of 49 CFR Part 393 Subpart I; the same headings satisfy the daily walkaround in the DVSA Guide to Maintaining Roadworthiness. This tool applies one rule of its own: an out-of-service defect blocks the safe-to-operate declaration until a mechanic records “defects corrected” with their name. It stores everything in this browser only — nothing is uploaded. Not legal advice.
