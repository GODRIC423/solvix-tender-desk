Rule checks are a planning aid, not a compliance ruling. This tool tests the rota you have typed against the three thresholds you set below — daily rest, consecutive days and weekly hours — for this one week only. The defaults (11 hours rest, 6 consecutive days, 48 hours) reflect limits commonly applied under the UK Working Time Regulations 1998 and EU Directive 2003/88/EC, but the tool does not average hours over a 17-week reference period, and does not apply opt-outs, night-worker limits, young-worker rules, sector exemptions, collective agreements or your own contracts of employment. It is not legal advice — check your obligations with your HR or employment adviser.

Week total —

Paid hours for a shift = finish − start (plus 24 hours when the finish is at or before the start, i.e. an overnight shift) − unpaid break minutes. Shift cost = paid hours × the person's rate, rounded to 2 decimal places, and every total on this page is the sum of those rounded shift costs, so the rota and the payroll line add up exactly. Rest is measured from the finish of one shift to the start of the next shift that person works. Consecutive days and rest also look back into the previous week where it has been rostered. Headcount is the number of people with any shift that day, compared with the cover figure you set. Hours and thresholds here are the ones you enter — they are not statutory values and no reference-period averaging is applied. Data is saved only in this browser.
