Tier prices are derived from cost: margin on selling price uses price = cost ÷ (1 − margin), markup on cost uses price = cost × (1 + markup). Band prices are list price × (1 − band discount), converted at the single rate you enter and then put through the rounding rule; a price typed directly into a cell is published exactly as typed and is not re-rounded. Achieved margin is measured on the published price converted back at the same rate, so it reflects what rounding actually did. No tax, duty, freight, settlement discount, rebate or landed-cost element is modelled. Everything stays in this browser — nothing is uploaded.
