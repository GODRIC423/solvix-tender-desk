This is a record, not medical advice

This tool only writes down what a prescriber has already instructed. It gives no dosing advice whatsoever: it does not check doses, interactions, allergies or suitability, and it must never be used to start, stop or change a dose. Always follow the dispensing label and your prescriber. If you think a dose is wrong, a dose has been missed or you feel unwell, contact your GP, pharmacist or your local urgent care service. In an emergency call your emergency number.

Supply & refills

Days remaining are rounded down — never assume an extra day of supply.
What this tool assumes. It reproduces a prescriber's existing instructions and records what was actually taken. Adherence = doses recorded as taken (including delayed) ÷ doses that had fallen due in the period × 100; doses not yet due are excluded from both sides. Supply = quantity dispensed − (doses recorded since the dispensing date × units per dose); days remaining = remaining ÷ units used per day, rounded down; reorder date = run-out date − lead time. Every figure comes from what you enter. No dosing advice is given or implied, no clinical checking is performed, and nothing here replaces the dispensing label, the patient information leaflet, your GP or your pharmacist. Everything stays in this browser — nothing is sent anywhere.
