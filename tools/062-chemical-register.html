Important notice. This register is a record of the hazard information entered by the named assessor. It is not, by itself, a COSHH assessment: it does not classify substances, calculate exposure or judge whether the controls listed are adequate. Every hazard classification, H/P statement and control measure must come from the supplier's current safety data sheet, and a suitable and sufficient assessment must be made and reviewed by a competent person (Control of Substances Hazardous to Health Regulations 2002, reg. 6). The storage compatibility check works from hazard class alone and is a prompt to check, not a substitute for a storage assessment. In an emergency, follow the safety data sheet and call the emergency services.

Chemical & COSHH Register. Hazard pictograms and H/P statement codes follow the GHS as implemented by CLP (Regulation (EC) No 1272/2008 as retained in GB law); statement wording is the standard Annex III/IV text. Safety data sheet ageing is measured in calendar months from the SDS revision date against the review period you set (12 months by default) — there is no legal fixed expiry, but suppliers must re-issue an SDS when it changes, so a periodic re-check is normal practice. Storage segregation follows the general principles in HSE guidance HSG71 (Chemical warehousing) and HSG51 (Storage of flammable liquids in containers); it is a prompt to check, not a substitute for your own storage assessment. Quantities are totalled by unit family — litres and millilitres as volume, kilograms and grams as mass, counted items separately — and are shown to 2 decimal places.
