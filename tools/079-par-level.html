Sheet details

Usage rate for every product is worked out from the change between the previous count and this one, over the number of days you set above. Order quantity = par level minus current count, rounded up to whole packs.

A supplier below its minimum order value is flagged — add lines, raise par on a product from that supplier, or hold the order until next count.

The par-right flag is a guide, not a stock policy. It is worked out from a single count-to-count usage rate, which can be thrown off by one busy or quiet period, a delivery logged in the wrong place, spoilage or wastage, seasonality, and promotions. It does not account for shelf life — do not raise a par on a perishable item just because this sheet suggests it. Treat every flag as a prompt to look at the line, not an instruction, and have a manager review the order before it is placed.

Totals ——— — ——
Usage/day = (previous count + received − current count) ÷ days since last count. Cover at par = par ÷ usage per day — how long par would last at the current rate. Order = par − current count, rounded up to whole packs.

Order quantity = (par level − current count) rounded up to the next whole pack; a product at or above par is not ordered. Usage per day = (previous count + received since − current count) ÷ days since last count, floored at zero. A product is flagged stockout risk when its current count would run out before the lead time on a new order elapses; overstocked when the par level holds more than four times the lead time's worth of stock at the current usage rate; otherwise it is left unflagged. These are heuristics from one usage sample, not a forecast or a reorder-point model — review before ordering. Money in this sheet is not VAT/tax adjusted; enter costs on whatever basis you buy at and be consistent. Everything stays in this browser until you export it.
