What's inside

Everything gleams. Everything flows.

Seven years of refinement, poured into one release. Each part of AquaOS 7 was redrawn to feel like light passing through water — and rebuilt underneath to be quicker than anything we've shipped.

Aqua Dock

A strip of clear plastic that holds your favourite apps. Icons swell as you approach and bounce once when they wake. Drag anything onto it — a folder, a song, a friend — and it stays.

Bubble Browser

Tabs are bubbles. Pull one out to make a window, push it back to merge. The Ripple engine paints text first so pages read instantly, even at 56k. Pop-ups are blocked before they surface.

Dewdrop Search

Press ⌘ Space and start typing. Files, mail, songs, contacts and settings ripple in before you reach the third letter, indexed quietly while your PowerFin sleeps.

Splash Mail

Conversations gather into gentle threads; people you actually talk to float to the top. A junk filter learns from what you drag away and keeps your inbox clear as a spring pool.

AquaTunes

Rip, mix and stream from one shining jukebox. The visualizer draws your music as rising bubbles, and playlists sync to a PocketFin over FireLink faster than you can find your headphones.

Instant wake

Close the lid, open it, keep working. AquaOS 7 wakes in 1.8 seconds with every window where you left it. The battery droplet in the menu bar fills and empties like the real thing.

AquaOS 7 is generous with older hardware. If your machine shipped in the last four years, it almost certainly qualifies — and the installer checks for you before touching a single file.

Memory256 MB RAM
Storage3 GB free on the startup disk
GraphicsAny 16 MB card; translucency falls back to soft tints
Display1024 × 768, thousands of colours
Install mediaCD-ROM drive or a 340 MB download

Recommended Full gloss

Memory512 MB RAM (1 GB for AquaTunes visualizer at full screen)
Storage8 GB free — leaves room for Dewdrop's index and Meadow wallpapers
