Design intent

A bicycle for two-hundred-kilometre days. The low-trail front end lets a loaded handlebar bag steer neutrally, the 650B wheel with a wide supple tyre floats over broken lanes at low pressure, and the sub-compact double keeps the cadence steady on long cols without a triple. Every fitting is brazed to the frame so nothing rattles loose at three in the morning.

General notes

  1. All dimensions in millimetres unless otherwise noted; angles in degrees to one decimal place.
  2. Geometry measured unloaded with tyres at 2.6 bar. Trail figure assumes the 42 mm tyre; a 38 mm tyre raises trail to 45.
  3. Frame alignment within 0.5 mm across the dropout faces; rear triangle centred on the seat tube.
  4. Minimum clearance between tyre and fender 12 mm; between fender and frame 6 mm.
  5. Handlebar bag load not to exceed 4 kg; rear saddlebag not to exceed 6 kg.
  6. Do not scale from this drawing. Where the drawing and the specification disagree, the specification governs.
