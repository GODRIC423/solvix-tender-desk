What this is

A process monitor for the machine you actually run on. Every line in pane 2 is something that is currently scheduled on you: sleep, coffee, worry, laundry, rent. CPU% is how much of your attention a thing is consuming right now; MEM% is how much of you it holds even when it is not running. TIME+ is the cumulative time it has had since boot, which was your birthday. Values refresh every second because that is how often they change.

The four meters at the top are your cores: body, mind, heart and wallet. Mem is attention. Swp is sleep debt, and if it is ever above zero you already know why.

States

R
running — it has you right now
S
sleeping — waiting on a timer or a person
D
uninterruptible — the laundry on the chair
T
stopped — the side project
Z
zombie — finished but still on your mind

Killing pid 1 is not permitted. Nothing here is saved; reload and it all comes back, which is also how mornings work.
