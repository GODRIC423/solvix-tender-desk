02 The program

Eleven days,
stepped out like a plan

Every event begins on the hour and ends when the ice does. Tours depart from the shaded side of the lot; lectures are in the Ocotillo Room; film nights happen on grass, so bring something to sit on. Filter by day, then tap the plus to keep an event in your itinerary.

Thu 12 · House tour9:00 AM

Kestrel House, before the heat

The first tour of the festival walks the butterfly roof from underneath, where the two shed planes meet in a rainwater trough nobody in the desert expected to need. Ninety minutes, small groups, shoes off in the sunken lounge.

1240 Vista Ridge

Thu 12 · Lecture1:00 PM

Who paid for the shade?

Historian Marguerite Oyelaran on the developers, the aerospace money and the mail-order steel that made a modernist tract house cheaper than a Spanish revival bungalow — for exactly fourteen years.

Ocotillo Room

Thu 12 · Cocktail hour5:30 PM

Opening night, poolside

The Tamarisk Shell throws the doors wide. Two bars, one trio, and a rule enforced since 1961: no drink may be carried up the diving board.

Tamarisk Shell

Fri 13 · House tour10:00 AM

Cordelia Ranch & its folded plate

Eleven concrete folds spanning forty feet with no interior column. The current owners have kept the terrazzo, lost the avocado kitchen, and are gracious about being asked why.

88 Cordelia Loop

Fri 13 · Panel2:30 PM

Breeze-block: a defence

Four architects argue about the decorative concrete screen — structure, ornament, air conditioning, or all three. Expect the pattern catalogue to be passed around and not returned.

Ocotillo Room

Fri 13 · Film on the lawn8:00 PM

Desert Modern, 16mm

Restored promotional reels from three developers, screened without commentary and with a great deal of laughter. Forty minutes. Blankets provided while they last.

Vista Bonita lawn

Sat 14 · Double tour8:30 AM

The Sandpiper, both wings

Rarely opened. A 96-foot screen wall of cast blocks throws a moving pattern across the entry court all morning — arrive early, it is the entire point of the building.

4 Sandpiper Ct

Sat 14 · Workshop11:00 AM

Draw a butterfly roof

Two hours with drafting pencils, a parallel rule and a very patient instructor. No experience needed; you leave with a section drawing and strong opinions about gutters.

Studio B

Sat 14 · Cocktail hour6:00 PM

Boomerang & bitters

Our long table under the carport of Casa Ocotillo. Five drinks, one of them non-alcoholic and better than the rest. Tickets capped at ninety.

Casa Ocotillo

Sun 15 · Walking tour9:30 AM

Six blocks, forty carports

A slow street-level walk through the Sunmark tract, counting roof pitches and arguing about which garages were original. Flat route, ninety minutes, ends at coffee.

Meet at the water tower

Sun 15 · Lecture12:00 PM

Ynez Marrero, in her own drawings

The first public showing of forty sheets from the Marrero office: hyperbolic shells, a shopping centre that was never built, and a house drawn nine times before anyone approved it.

Ocotillo Room

Sun 15 · Closing4:00 PM

Last call at the Shell

The festival ends the way it started, in the shade of a concrete paraboloid, with the trio playing the same four songs and everyone pretending not to look at the time.

Tamarisk Shell

03 The tour map

Six houses,
one hot valley

All six are private homes opened by their owners for the week only. Choose a marker on the map or a panel below — they are wired to each other. Addresses are released with your pass; please do not photograph the neighbours.

Select a house

Six markers, one valley

Hover or tab through the markers to read each house. The ridge is west, the highway east, and every carport in between points at the afternoon sun.

04 Five thirty, sharp

The cocktail
hour

It started as a builder's open house in 1961 with warm gin and a card table. It is now the hardest ticket of the week, and the gin is kept in a chest freezer behind the carport.

Five drinks are poured each evening. Four are historically defensible. The fifth is what the bartender feels like making, and it is always the one that runs out.

Add to a pass

  • The Butterfly1961Dry gin, lime cordial, a hard shake and two drops of saline. Served in a coupe so cold it fogs the moment it leaves the freezer.
  • Breeze-Block1964Blanco tequila, avocado-leaf syrup, grapefruit and soda, poured over one square block of clear ice with a hole through the middle.
  • Burnt Orange Negroni1958Equal parts, stirred, with a wide peel scorched over the flame until the whole carport smells of it.
  • Terrazzo FizzNon-alcoholicSeville shrub, tonic, bitters and speckles of candied peel that settle out like aggregate. The one we make the most of.
  • The Bartender's WhimNightlyWritten on a card at 5:29 PM. Gone by 6:15 PM. Do not ask for it at 6:16 PM.
