Our story

It began with a blob that would not stop bouncing.

In 2021 Ingrid Solheim was prototyping ergonomic grips for a hardware client when a batch of foam came back wrong: too soft, too slow, faintly lavender. The client rejected it. Her niece did not. The rejected blob went home in a coat pocket and came back a week later with a name.

Blob & Co. still pours every toy in the same small studio in Grünerløkka. The foam is cast from sugar-beet polyols instead of petroleum, tinted with food-safe pigment, and cured slowly so the skin stays matte and the inside stays springy. There are no seams, no painted faces to peel and no parts smaller than a fist. A toy that has been dropped, chewed, thrown at a sibling and left in the rain should look exactly like it did in the box. Every one carries a five-year squish guarantee: if it stops bouncing back, send it home and we will replace it.
