How the operation works

We do the smuggling. You do the wincing.

Interrogation room

Questions we get asked

Everything you'd want to know before letting strangers mail you fish.

+Is any of this actually legal?
Entirely. Every item is a shelf-stable, commercially packaged snack purchased at retail and declared at the border. "Cartel" is a brand, not a business model. Our lawyer made us say that and also made us stop calling shipments "drops."
+What if I have allergies?
Every crate includes a dossier with the full ingredient list and allergen statement for each snack, translated into English by a human. You can also set hard exclusions (nuts, gluten, fish, dairy, sesame) in your account and we will swap items for you. We take this part seriously, which is the only part we take seriously.
+When does my first crate ship?
Crates leave the warehouse on the 1st of every month. Join before the 25th and you're in that month's haul; join after and you'll get the next one. Delivery is 3 to 7 days in most countries. Durian is double-bagged. Nobody has complained twice.
+Can I skip a month or cancel?
Yes and yes. Skip from your account with one click, cancel with two. No phone calls, no retention specialist named Brandon, no "are you sure" that lasts longer than the snack did. Yearly plans can be paused up to three times.
+Do you take suggestions?
We beg for them. Lieutenants and Kingpins vote on next month's target country, and anyone can nominate a snack. If we ship something you found, you get a free crate and your name in the zine under "Informant."
