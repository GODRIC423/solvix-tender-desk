    NOTE 1

    Read the board

    This is the top-side silkscreen of the controller that sits inside every Solvix tender desk. Everything the desk does — the USB link to the operator terminal, the CAN bus to the bid printers, the four analogue channels that watch the drawer sensors — passes through the copper you can see above. Gold pulses ride each trace in the direction the signal actually travels: power flows from J1 through the regulator toward U1; debug flows from J3 upward; sensor data leaves J4 and arrives at the ADC.

    Traces drawn in bright copper are on layer 1. Darker copper is layer 4, seen through the board, and is only used where a route has to cross the 3V3 rail without a via farm — the two expansion lines to J5 and the 3V3 feed to U5. Layers 2 and 3 are solid ground and power pours and are not drawn.

    Hover or tab to any component to highlight the nets it touches and open its datasheet summary. Work the other way round from the net rail above the board: pick CAN_BUS and every segment of that net lights, from the transceiver through the split termination to the terminal block, along with each part sitting on it. Hover a line in the bill of materials or the revision history and the same highlighting appears on the board. Click to pin, press Escape to release, and use the Signals control in the header if you need a still board.

    BLOCK 2

    Revision history

    CReleased
    2026-08-19ECO-11 → ECO-15
    Drawn · M. Okonkwo
    Checked · R. Lindqvist

    Thermal and field-return fixes after six months of Rev B in production desks. Board outline, connector positions and mounting holes are unchanged so Rev C drops into the existing enclosure.

    • ECO-11U2 regulator changed from a 1 A linear part to a TPS62933 synchronous buck. Idle temperature at the regulator fell from 71 °C to 38 °C in the closed desk.
    • ECO-12U4 replaced by an isolated ADM3055E transceiver. Desks sharing a CAN trunk across two mains phases no longer lose the bus when ground offsets exceed 2 V.
    • ECO-13R1 · R2 single 120 Ω termination split into two 60.4 Ω halves with a centre tap for the shield, cutting common-mode ringing at the printer end.
    • ECO-14C1 input bulk raised from 22 µF to 47 µF; the buck's input ripple at 3 A load is now inside the USB-C source spec.
    • ECO-15U3 QSPI lines length-matched to ± 0.5 mm and moved to the top layer only; the 133 MHz read mode is now enabled in firmware.
    BSuperseded
    2026-03-02ECO-04 → ECO-10
    Drawn · M. Okonkwo
    Checked · A. Ferreira

    Move to four layers and the current connector set. First revision shipped in production desks.

    • ECO-04J1 micro-B replaced by a USB-C receptacle with through-hole retention pegs after socket tear-outs in service.
    • ECO-06D1 TVS added on VBUS; three Rev A boards had failed regulators after hot-plug on long cables.
    • ECO-08U5 ADS131M04 added with J4 so the drawer load cells are read on the board rather than by the terminal.
    • ECO-10Stack-up changed from two to four layers with a solid ground plane on L2; USB pair routed as a 90 Ω controlled-impedance differential pair.
    APrototype
    2025-11-14Initial release
    Drawn · M. Okonkwo
    Checked · —

    Two-layer prototype, ten boards, hand-assembled. Proved the STM32G4 plus FDCAN architecture and the desk firmware, and found most of the problems the later revisions fix.

    • —Linear 3V3 regulator, non-isolated CAN transceiver, micro-B USB, no on-board ADC. Crystal footprint and debug header carried forward unchanged to Rev C.
