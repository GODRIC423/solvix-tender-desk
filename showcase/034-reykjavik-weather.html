Forecaster's note

Why tonight clears when the model said it wouldn't

HRHalla Rún · evening desk, 20:55

The afternoon runs kept low cloud parked over the city until Friday, but the occlusion that brought the dusk showers is moving faster than expected. Cold air behind it is dry, and the westerly off Faxaflói has already carved a widening gap in the stratocumulus over Akranes. We now expect a clear slot from roughly 23:00 to 03:00 before high cloud ahead of Friday's warm front arrives from the south-west.

That slot is the story of the night. With Kp forecast at 4 and the moon not rising until close to midnight, the first hour of darkness offers the best combination of geomagnetic activity and a dark sky we have had since March. Wind chill is the price: 9 m/s at 4° feels like freezing, so this is a night for the thermos and the wool.

Friday turns wet by lunchtime and stays that way. Sunday and Monday are the ones to circle for anything outdoors.
