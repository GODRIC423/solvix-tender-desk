from the shoeboxSummer '89

Twelve pictures, one Volvo, four people who did not know how good it was. Nobody threw these out. They just stayed in the box, shifting around every time somebody moved house.

– the one holding the camera

First night. Pier at 9pm

June 17

Drove straight from graduation with the seats still folded down. Dee swore the pier would be closed. It was not closed. We ate fries out of a paper cone and nobody said anything about September.

roll 1 / shot 1
The pool at the Sandpiper

Sandpiper Motel

One working ice machine and a pool shaped like a kidney. Marco did the cannonball that got us the written warning. The manager kept the towel as evidence. We kept the room key by accident.

June 20
Bonfire, night three

Flash was off

You cannot really see the fire. That is Priya's shoulder on the left and the shape that might be the moon. We ran out of wood at midnight and burned the paper plates, then the map.

June 22
Route 9, somewhere

Tape deck incident

The deck ate the Blondie cassette right about here. Marco pulled it out one loop at a time, a hundred miles of brown ribbon out the window. We sang the rest from memory, badly, in the wrong key.

June 30
Starlite Drive-In

Double feature, $4 a car

So we put six people in the Volvo. Nobody remembers the second movie because the speaker fell off the window halfway through and we watched it silent, making up the lines.

July 1
Lake, 6am, everyone asleep

Was the mist real?

I got up to check. It was. Took this standing in the cold water up to my ankles, then went back to the tent and slept until noon. Nobody believed me until the roll came back.

July 3
Dee's birthday at Frosty's

Nineteen

Frosty's gave her a free cone for the occasion and she dropped it in under a minute. The second one she held with both hands the whole way back to the car and would not speak to anyone.

July 4, morning
Fourth of July from the roof

Tar roof, still warm

The whole town went off at once. Priya said it looked like a bad tie. Best seats we ever had, and the roof stayed warm past midnight so nobody wanted to climb down.

July 4, night
The towel we all fought over

The only big towel

Rules were made. Rules were broken. A rota was drawn up on a napkin and lost. As far as I know it is still in the trunk of the Volvo, sandy and undefeated.

most of July
Storm coming over the corn

We outran it for twenty minutes

Then gave up and stood in it. Loudest thing I have ever heard. The camera was inside my shirt the whole time, which is why the corner is wavy.

Aug 9
Lighthouse in the fog (trust me)

There is a lighthouse here

It is the pale bar in the middle. We paid two dollars each to climb it and saw exactly this from the top, only wetter. Marco bought the postcard so we would know what it looked like.

Aug 15
Last night. Porch light on.

Aug 28

Nobody wanted to say it so nobody did. We left the light on all night. Dee drove off first. The Volvo followed. I stood there until the film ran out, which was this one.
