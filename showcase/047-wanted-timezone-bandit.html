The Charges

  1. The Great Hour Robbery of the Nine-Fifteen

    Boarded the westbound mail at the water stop and relieved it of a full sixty minutes between two tunnels. Passengers stepped down onto the platform before they had finished boarding it.

    Cheyenne Junction · Spring of ’83

  2. Larceny of Daylight

    Took one hour from every household in the county on a Sunday morning and returned it, unrepentant, in the autumn. No court has yet decided whether this constitutes theft or a loan.

    Meridian County · Twice yearly, ongoing

  3. Interference with a Public Clock

    Climbed the tower at Kirkwall Flats and turned the great hand back a quarter-turn, causing the noon gun to fire into an afternoon that had not been declared open.

    Kirkwall Flats · 4th of August

  4. Grand Theft of a Wednesday

    An entire Wednesday absent from the ledger of the Overland Line. The bookkeeper swears he wrote Tuesday in the morning and Thursday after lunch, and will not be persuaded otherwise.

    Dry Fork Station · Date disputed

  5. Crossing the Date Line with Intent

    Fled east across the line and gained a day on his pursuers, then re-crossed it westward and spent that day in a saloon where he had already been arrested the evening before.

    Beyond the 180th Meridian

A Word from the Marshal

To all marshals, station agents and keepers of clocks: the man above is not to be approached at any hour he has been given notice of. He is polite. He is punctual to a fault. He is unarmed but for a pair of brass callipers and a small ledger in which he writes down what he has taken and what he intends to take next.

Twice we have had him in the Kirkwall cells. Twice the morning has arrived without him in it — once with the cell door still bolted and the guard's watch running eleven minutes slow. Do not set your timepiece by anything he says. Do not accept a ride on any coach he has already boarded.

If you sight him, wire this office at once — and wire it twice: once from your hour and once from ours, so that we may triangulate the hour he is standing in. The reward below is honest and it grows nightly, being reckoned against the minutes still owed to the public.

J. A. MerrowMarshal · Territory of the Twenty-Four Meridians

Sightings to be wired to: The Marshal's Office, Kirkwall Flats, Wire No. 4 — state the hour twice.
