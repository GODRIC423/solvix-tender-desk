How to play

Click or tap any white square and start typing. Space swaps between Across and Down, Tab jumps to the next unfinished clue, Shift+Tab steps back, and the arrow keys walk the grid one square at a time. Backspace rubs out and reverses.

Switch on Pencil when you are only guessing: pencilled letters print grey and italic, but they are graded exactly the same as ink. Check puts a slash through every wrong letter already in the grid and leaves the empty squares alone. Reveal fills the entry you are sitting in and prints it in gold, so that later you will know which ones you did not earn.

      A note from the setter

      This is a Sunday grid in the older sense: nothing in it needs a reference book, only a second cup of coffee and a willingness to read a clue twice. The four five-letter columns went in first, the south-east corner argued with me for most of a week, and 27 Down eventually named itself, which felt like the puzzle agreeing to stop.

      If a clue reads like a small joke told across a breakfast table, that is deliberate. The grid is fully checked — every white square belongs to one Across answer and one Down answer — so no letter is ever left to guesswork alone. Solvers who finish in under six minutes are asked, politely, not to mention it to the rest of the table.
