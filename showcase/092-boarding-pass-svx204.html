01Before you board

Boarding for SVX 204 opens at 07:05 at the gate printed above and closes at 07:30, fifteen minutes before departure. You are in boarding zone 3, which is called after Solvix Gold members and zones 1 and 2. Have this pass and photo ID ready at the gate. A screen and a print are equally valid, but the barcode must be legible, so raise your brightness or keep the printed stub flat and dry.

Your Flex fare includes one cabin bag up to 8 kg (55 × 40 × 23 cm) and one personal item that fits under the seat in front. Bags that do not fit the gauge at the gate are tagged and carried in the hold at no charge on this flight, but they come out on the belt in Lisbon, so keep medication, documents and electronics with you.

Oslo and Lisbon are both inside the Schengen area, so there is no passport control on arrival, only a random customs check. Keep your ID anyway; the crew may ask for it when matching names to seats.

02About this flight

SVX 204 is Solvix Air's morning rotation between Oslo Gardermoen and Lisbon Humberto Delgado, flown by an Airbus A321neo with four rows of business seating and twenty-eight rows of economy in a 3-3 layout. Block time is 4 hours 25 minutes. The routing runs south over the Skagerrak and the Danish coast, crosses the Low Countries and the Bay of Biscay, and joins the Lisbon arrival over Peniche.

A hot breakfast is served in business; economy has a buy-on-board menu, and the seat-back pocket holds the route map and the safety card, which is worth reading even if you have flown this type before.

Sunrise over the North Sea is at about 06:40 at this time of year, so a window seat on the left (A) catches the early light, while the right side (F) keeps the coastline for most of the second half.

03Gate changes

Gate assignments at Oslo are confirmed roughly ninety minutes before departure and can move if the inbound aircraft parks at a different stand. When that happens this pass updates itself: the gate digits flip to the new gate and an orange notice appears at the top of the document with the walking time between piers.

Gate B14 and gate C22 are on different piers. Allow nine minutes between them, more if you are travelling with a child seat or need assistance, which you can request from any Solvix agent in the pier. The boarding time does not change unless the notice says so.

Once the stub is torn, keep it until you have collected your bag in Lisbon; it carries the sequence number the ground staff use to trace a missing bag.
