SOLITAIRE DELUXE 1.0 — README

Rookery Software, September 1996 Thank you for installing Solitaire Deluxe. This file contains information that was not available when the manual went to the printer.

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS

A 486DX or better processor, 8 MB of RAM, a VGA display capable of 256 colours, 4.8 MB of free disk space, and a mouse. A sound card is optional but the victory cascade is much better with one.

GAMES INCLUDED

Klondike (draw one or draw three), Spider (one, two or four suits), FreeCell, Pyramid, Golf, Clock and Forty Thieves. Each game keeps its own statistics and its own longest winning streak.

KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS

F2 deals a new game. Ctrl+Z undoes the last move; up to 200 moves are remembered. F3 opens the statistics ledger. Ctrl+H shows a hint by flashing the card that can be moved. Pressing the space bar turns the next stock card.

KNOWN ISSUES

On some displays set to 16 colours the "Ocean" and "Night Sky" card backs look identical. This is a limitation of the display, not of the ocean. If the Auto-Finish feature is used while a card is being dragged, the card will complete its journey on its own. This is intentional but has been reported as a ghost. FreeCell deal #11982 remains unsolved by the development team. We have stopped trying.

CONTACT

Write to Rookery Software, Suite 4, The Old Print Works, Bristol. Please include your version number and a description of the deal you were playing.
