What leaves this shop

Everything here is drawn out under a hammer, not cut from a sheet or poured into a mould. Three lines of work; one standard. If it would not survive a hundred winters, it does not leave the yard.

Line oneKnives

Kitchen, butchery and field blades forged from 80CrV2 and 52100 bar. Each blade is hand-drawn to profile, normalised three times, quenched in warm oil and tempered twice. Handles in stabilised oak, bog ash or blackened brass, pinned with our own rivets.

Ingot to blade

Steel tells you what it is doing by colour. Read the bar as it heats and you know when to strike, when to wait and when to quench. Seven stages, one continuous heat.

  1. Stage 01

    Ingot

    Cold 80CrV2 bar, 6 mm thick, cut to length and stamped with the batch number.

    20°C · Grey
  2. Stage 02

    Heat

    Into the coke fire, tuyere open. The bar dulls, then blooms a first faint red in the shadow of the hood.

    650°C · Dull red
  3. Stage 03

    Draw

    Bright cherry. The bar is drawn out on the anvil horn, gaining length and losing thickness with every heat.

    850°C · Cherry
  4. Stage 04

    Shape

    Orange to yellow. Tip, bevels and tang are set with the cross-peen; the profile appears in six heats.

    1,050°C · Yellow
  5. Stage 05

    Normalise

    Three cycles to a lemon heat and a slow air cool refine the grain and erase the stress of forging.

    1,150°C · Lemon
  6. Stage 06

    Quench

    Back to a non-magnetic red, then into warm oil. Two seconds of steam and the steel is glass-hard.

    820°C · Red
  7. Stage 07

    Temper

    Two hours at straw-brown in the oven, twice, trading a little hardness for a blade that flexes and returns.

    200°C · Straw

Commissioning a piece

We take on around thirty commissions a year and keep the book honest. Here is how it goes.

  1. Talk. A conversation at the forge or by phone about the site, the use and the look you are after.
  2. Draw. A chalk drawing on the shop floor at full size. You are welcome to stand on it and argue.
  3. Forge. A deposit of a third books your slot in the fire. You will get photographs at every heat.
  4. Fit. We hang, fix and finish on site, and come back once a year to oil it if you want us to.
