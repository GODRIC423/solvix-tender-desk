Where would you
like to go?

Eight galleries, one loop.

Level 1 is a single ring corridor. Walk it clockwise or anti-clockwise and you will pass every gallery door and end up back at the Entrance Hall, where you started.

Getting around

Step-free everywhere

Every gallery on this level is on one floor with no steps. The lift is at the north-west corner of the corridor, beside Gallery 1, and serves all three levels. Wheelchairs and folding stools can be borrowed free of charge from the Entrance Hall desk.

Toilets and changing places

Toilets, including an accessible toilet and a Changing Places room, are at the north-east corner, beside Gallery 3. Baby-changing is available in every toilet.

Café and cloakroom

The Foundry Café is at the south-east corner beside Gallery 5, serving from 10:00 until 30 minutes before closing. Coats, bags and umbrellas can be left free of charge at the cloakroom inside the Entrance Hall.

Quiet hour

Every Wednesday from 10:00 to 11:00 the working machines are switched off, lighting is lowered and visitor numbers are limited. The Quiet Room is inside Gallery 8, the Sculpture Court.

Opening hours

Tuesday to Sunday 10:00 to 17:00, last entry 16:15. Closed on Mondays except bank holidays. The museum is free; donations keep the beam engine in steam.

Need a hand?

Look for a Visitor Host in a yellow lanyard. They can walk with you to any gallery, describe objects, and lend large-print and audio guides in eleven languages.
