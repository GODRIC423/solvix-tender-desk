    Notes Read before entering the sound

    Cautions Dangers and their marks

    The Teeth, a group of drying rocks four cables south of the recommended track, are steep-to and give no warning on the echo-sounder; the north cardinal buoy placed off their northern edge is the only mark. Solvix Middle Ground (least depth 1·6 m) uncovers at extreme low water springs and is guarded on its north-western edge by No 5 buoy. A wreck with 2·8 m over it lies on the northern edge of Kalder Bank; the isolated danger mark on it has been reported to drag in easterly gales and should not be relied upon.

    Depths in Kalder Gut are liable to change. The shoalest sounding found in the survey of 2025 is charted; mariners are asked to report discrepancies to the Solvix Hydrographic Office.

    Tidal streams Referred to HW Solvix Head

    Streams in the sound set along the axis of the fairway. The in-going stream begins about 1 h 10 min before high water and attains 2·4 knots at springs in Kalder Gut, where overfalls form with wind against tide. The out-going stream begins about twenty minutes after high water and runs at up to 1·8 knots. Between Merrow Isle and the fairway buoy a cross-set of half a knot is felt at the turn of the tide; make good the track, not the heading.

    Pilotage Recommended track

    Approach from the south-west with Solvix Head light bearing 032° until the fairway buoy is abeam to port, then steer to pass midway between No 2 and No 3 buoys. From Mid-Sound the leading line — Kalder Ness light in transit with the white beacon on Gull Skerry, bearing 066° — clears the Teeth by a cable. Small craft may pass south of the Teeth in daylight with local knowledge; the passage is not recommended after dark. Anchorage may be had in Kalder Roads in 10 to 14 m, mud and shell, good holding, sheltered from all but easterly winds.
