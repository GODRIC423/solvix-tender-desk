xHouse rules

  1. No fascists. No racists. No exceptions. Out the door, no refund, and the door staff have long memories.
  2. Look after each other. If someone goes down in the pit, pick them up before the next chord. Then get back in.
  3. Phones away during the set. Watch it with your eyes. The video would be terrible anyway; the room has two lights and one of them flickers.
  4. No refunds. No encores. No requests. They do not know any covers, and they will not learn one for you.
  5. The ceiling is low. Tall people, mind the pipe by the stairs. It has drawn blood before and it is not sorry.
  6. Take your cans home with you. The laundrette opens at seven and Marguerite has been patient enough.
  7. If the power trips, keep shouting. The drummer will keep playing. He always does.
  8. Photocopy this flyer. Stick it somewhere it should not be. Rip it down after the gig.

Who are Dead Pixels

Four people from the wrong end of the ring road who formed in the back room of a phone repair shop in the winter of 2023, after the shop closed and nobody came to collect the sign. The name is the only thing they agreed on in the first month. The singer used to fix the screens. The bassist used to break them.

They have one EP, Screen Burn, recorded in a single afternoon on a borrowed four-track that has since been returned in worse condition. The live set runs twenty-two minutes on a good night and eighteen on a bad one. Nobody in the band can read music. This is not an apology; it is a warning about the tempo.

This is their last show before the Basement gets turned into storage for the laundrette upstairs. Come and shout at them while there is still a floor to stand on.
