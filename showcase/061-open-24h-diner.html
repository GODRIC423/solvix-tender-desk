The Menu

Breakfast, all day served 24h · yes, at 4pm

  • The Blue Plate$11.50

    Two eggs any style, hash browns pressed flat until they shatter, a biscuit, and your pick of bacon, link sausage, or a slab of country ham. The plate is blue. That is the whole gimmick.

  • Short Stack, Long Night$8.75

    Three buttermilk pancakes the size of hubcaps, whipped butter, warm syrup in a tin pitcher. Add blueberries or chocolate chips for a dollar, nobody judges.

  • Biscuits & Sausage Gravy$9.25

    Split biscuits under a ladle of black-pepper gravy made from the morning's sausage drippings. Heavy enough to anchor a truck.

  • Denver Omelette$10.95

    Three eggs folded around ham, green pepper, onion and cheddar. Comes with toast and a side of hash browns because the omelette gets lonely.

  • Hangover Hash$12.00

    Diced potatoes, brisket ends, peppers and onions fried on the flat-top, topped with two sunny eggs and hot sauce. Most popular between 1 and 3 a.m. for reasons we don't ask about.

✦Any breakfast comes with a bottomless cup. Eggs from Harlan Farm up the road; they know the hens by name.

  • Pie of the Night$5.50

Coffee & the counter refills are free · we will keep pouring until you cover the cup

  • Bottomless Drip$2.50

    Medium roast, brewed in twelve-minute batches so it never goes bitter on the burner. One price, all night, endless. Our record is nineteen refills, held by a trucker named Ines since 2011.

  • Counter Melt$9.50

    Patty melt on rye with grilled onions and Swiss, griddle-pressed and cut on the diagonal. Fries or slaw, or a cup of soup if you're being sensible.

  • Blue Plate Chili$6.75

    Beef and bean, simmered since noon, with crackers and a shaker of cheddar. Bowls only. Nobody has ever wanted a cup.

  • Thick Shakes$6.00

    Vanilla, chocolate, strawberry, or malt, spun in the green mixer and served with the steel can. A spoon comes standard.
