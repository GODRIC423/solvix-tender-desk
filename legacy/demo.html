Connecting TRUXCO is a settings change

The interchange already builds and validates. What is not yet known is how TRUXCO wants a handful of qualifiers filled in. Work down this list with their EDI coordinator, then switch the transport on and flip the usage indicator to production.

Confirm with their 204 guide

SettingCurrent value
ISA06 sender idSOLVIXLOG
ISA08 receiver idTRUXCO
GS02 / GS03SOLVIX / TRUXCO
Our SCAC (B2-02)SOMA
Money format in L3-05implied 2 decimals
N7-24 length unitinches
G62 date qualifierstender_date=10, pickup_earliest=37, pickup_latest=38, delivery_earliest=53, delivery_latest=54
G62 time qualifierI
Miles reference qualifierZZ
Equipment code mapvan=TV, reefer=RT, flatbed=FT, container=CN, tanker=TK, straight=SV
Usage indicator (ISA15)T

Then, in order

1Point the transport at their SFTP, AS2 endpoint or API.
2Send 5–10 real tenders in test mode: single stop, multi-stop, reefer, hazmat, and one with an accessorial.
3Have them return a 997 for each and confirm the order landed correctly in their order entry.
4Fix any qualifier mismatches in settings — not in code.
5Flip ISA15 from T to P. Nothing else changes.

Worth asking during onboarding: will they send 997 acknowledgments, and where will they drop them? Much easier to set up now than to retrofit later.
